Home

Young lady, be ambitious!

I am reading the course requirements on the sofa in front of the fireplace.

『I was never good at writing course registration forms,』 memories of university in my previous life come flooding back, but more importantly, it's time to decide on a course.

First, I'll pass on the Knight Course. I can't even ride a horse, so I can't become a knight.

「I wonder if I can take Daily Life Magic in the Mage Course? Huh, Alchemy?」

Since coming to this other world, I've been able to use magic, and I've seen it used in practical magic classes, but I didn't know about the scientific side of things.

「I see, making magic tools that boil water with magic stones, clean toilets, or provide light is what Alchemy is.」

The Civil Officer Course has law classes, but it also has interesting subjects like economics and foreign languages. Since I'm in another world anyway, I'd like to travel. Monsters are scary, but I'm sure there's protection or something like that.

The winters in the Kingdom of Lawrence are cold. When I get old, I want to live in a warmer climate. This might be a trauma from my first experience in this other world, where I almost died from the cold.

「But I'm also interested in alchemy. It's so romantic.」

「What are you interested in?」

I was startled.

「Princess Margaret, why are you here?」

I am surprised to see Princess Margaret sitting on the simple sofa in front of me, looking around the room with interest.

「You didn't come to my room even though I waited forever, and you didn't answer no matter how many times I knocked, so I let myself in. So, what are you interested in? Oh, the course requirements for the middle school division. You aren't thinking of skipping a grade to middle school, are you?」

「No, it's still a ways off, but I was thinking about which course to take in the middle school division.」

Princess Margaret smiled. I have nothing but a bad feeling about this.

「Say, Patience. I wonder if you couldn't skip a grade to middle school next year.」

I feel like a frog being stared at by a snake. I wonder if Princess Margaret has the same commanding presence as Prince Richard?

「If possible, I would like to...」

I'm an idiot! I'll end up being a classmate of Princess Margaret. I'm truly a fool.

「But I still have to study, and I'm not good at dancing...」

I break into a cold sweat and make excuses about how it's impossible.

「Oh, dancing has nothing to do with skipping a grade. But if you're not good at it, you just need to practice. So, what did you get interested in?」

Since it's come to this, I have no choice but to tell the truth.

「I was thinking of taking the Civil Officer Course, but I was drawn to the alchemy class in the Mage Course.」

Princess Margaret smiled broadly. Smiles are scary, aren't they?

「Even if you take the Civil Officer Course, you can still take alchemy classes. Besides, I'm in the Home Economics Course, so you could just take that too.」

I cling to Princess Margaret, who has a radiant expression.

「That's impossible. Taking both the Home Economics Course and the Civil Officer Course. I wouldn't be able to take alchemy then.」

「Well now, Patience. You've effectively already earned completion certificates in Practical Magic, Home Economics, and Music, haven't you? And you're planning to take Mathematics, aren't you, which is why you've already received the textbooks from the teacher?」

It was a mistake to let her into the room. I've been found out because of the middle school mathematics textbook.

「Besides, there are people who take two courses. My brother Richard is taking both the Knight Course and the Civil Officer Course, after all.」

I’m getting sick of that high-and-mighty Prince Richard. I actually felt a tiny bit of sympathy for Prince Keith.

"That’s great. Mother’s judgment is truly wonderful. This means we’ll be classmates next year, and since you're good enough to earn a certificate in mathematics, you’ll have to teach me."

Princess Margaret, in high spirits, dragged me to a special room, where she made me practice Hanon for my new song to my heart's content.

While my head was in a fog, I started thinking about how many subject certificates one would need to take two courses, and when I made a mistake on the touch, Princess Margaret scolded me. I feel like I'm having a string of bad luck.

"If you don't practice properly, you're going to embarrass yourself in front of Mother on Friday."

So it's true that you can be so surprised that your breathing stops. I felt like I was going to die twice over.

"What might you mean by that?" Patience's manners are perfect. Even when she feels like shouting, 'Wait, I didn't hear about this!', she converts it into a polite question.

"Oh, didn't I tell you? Mother sent a letter saying she wants to see the face of Patience, your lady-in-waiting. You'll be accompanying me when I go back on Friday."

Princess Margaret's words, like "You should perform a new piece," didn't even register in my head.

Seeing Nashius and Henry on the weekend is my only emotional support (oasis).

"Um, I really need to go home, so..."

My plea that I didn't want to go to the royal palace was ignored.

"I'll make sure you get home safely."

I don't know when that will be, but at least I'll be able to see my little brothers. I have no choice but to settle for that for now.

After that, I practiced desperately. Perhaps thanks to Patience cheering me on with, "Do your best!", I was able to play the Mozart sonata smoothly. I even got used to Hanon.

Dinner—when things aren't going your way, they really aren't. I ended up sitting at the same table as Prince Richard.

"Brother, Patience is aiming for the intermediate course next year. We'll be classmates."

Ah, I haven't even managed to skip a grade yet, and I wish she would stop isolating me from all sides. But as a lady-in-waiting, I have no choice but to stay silent and listen.

"Is that so? Patience, you really are excellent."

Even his words of praise felt like he was piling more dirt onto my grave.

"Thank you very much," I said, offering my thanks nonetheless.

"What's more, Patience even intends to earn a certificate in mathematics. She plans to take two courses: the home economics course and the civil service course."

Wow, she’s not just isolating me from the outside, but from the inside, too.

"I see. You have great ambitions. Do your best."

I don't have any great ambitions. I just wanted to skip a grade to get more time with my little brothers.
