A Shocking Dinner

There are still plenty of things to improve in my life, but my stomach is telling me it's time for dinner. So, I tried to head to the dining room, but Patience checked me.

『You must change your clothes before going to dinner!』

Now that I think about it, in British aristocratic dramas, they changed their clothes for dinner every time. Though those were luxurious feasts, I can't really expect a meal worth dressing up for here.

I take out the other dress hanging in the armoire in the dim room.

「It looks cold, though……」

It has long sleeves, but it's made of lace. It's so cold, lace seems inappropriate. It makes me want to cry.

「I wonder if it would be bad to wear clothes underneath?」

I was fiercely rejected by Patience with a 『No!』, and while shivering in the unheated room, I changed into the lace-sleeved dress.

「Ugh…… it's cold. I need to get to the dining room quickly……」

Patience is making a fuss, telling me to brush my hair and set it nicely.

『Geez, are you really dead, Patience? I feel like you have an awful lot of complaints.』

That said, my time as an office worker in Japan wasn't for nothing. Even if I overslept, I could get dressed, put on makeup, and leave my apartment in ten minutes. Looking into the fogged-up mirror of an old vanity that didn't reflect well, I brushed my hair, lifted a section, and put it in a half-up style. I fastened it with the only hair accessory I found in the drawer.

Blonde hair, blue eyes—I could say it's quite cute, but unfortunately, it's too thin. My eyes look a bit bulging too.

Never in my life did I think a day would come where I'd think, "I need to gain more weight!" I didn't even have time to indulge in such reflections.

「My Lady…… I am so sorry. Helping you dress is……」

Mary hurried in. Ah, according to Patience's memory, Mary used to help her change before dinner.

「It's fine.」 Yes, since I've been having Mary do everything for me.

But Mary really looks disappointed.

「Tomorrow, before we go to church, I will fix your hair.」

I see, she wants to do her job as a maid, too. Even though Mary also does the work of a housemaid, she is, originally, a maid.

I told her I'd leave it to her for tomorrow and hurried to the dining room. I tried to act cool and gave my thin slice of ham to my younger brothers, but my stomach was growling. Besides, even if it's early, there's a fireplace in the dining room.

My father was sitting in the dining room. Perhaps there is a dress code, as he was wearing a black jacket and a white tie. That black jacket was also worn thin at the elbows and shiny in places.

「Oh, Patience. Now then, let's begin dinner.」

At breakfast and lunch, my father remained seated, but the moment I entered the dining room dressed up, he stood up to greet me. I'd rather he do something to earn an income than keep such etiquette.

「But, Natius and Henry aren't here yet……」

A thin soup, the same as in the morning, was served by the butler. That, too, is different from breakfast and lunch. Usually, it was Mary. More importantly, only two sets of cutlery were laid out on the table.

「Lady Patience? The children will not be joining us for dinner.」

Wyatt, the butler, replied in a low voice to my agitation. I hurriedly Googled Patience's memories. It seems that children up to the age of 10 eat dinner in the nursery. In wealthy noble households, nannies feed them morning, noon, and night. In other words, because the Granger family is poor, I was able to eat in the morning and afternoon with

my younger brothers

together.

Thin soup, thin ham, and hard bread. So far, it was the same as lunch, but to my surprise, dessert was served.

"It is an apple, milady."

Thinly sliced apples accompanied by cheese. And then, wine for my father. Tea for me. It's so weak! Everything Eva cooks is weak. Room for improvement!

"Tomorrow at church, just calm your heart and take the ability test."

Conversations with my father never really take off. I really want to ask why we are so poor, but Patience has forbidden it. It seems a daughter cannot ask such rude questions to her father.

Unlike in the morning or at lunch, the fireplace fire wasn't extinguished immediately, but I left the dining room early because I was worried about my younger brothers. After all, there’s no nanny, and I'm worried if they were fed dinner or not.

In the nursery, my younger brothers were already in bed.

"Sister, did you come to see us?"

A candle was still lit by the bedside. It looked like they had just gotten into bed.

"Yes, I had to say goodnight to Nashius and Henry. Did you both eat your dinner properly?"

"Yeah, the apple was delicious."

"I love cheese."

It seems they are being fed properly after all. I'm relieved. Well, perhaps the servants who have stayed with the impoverished Granger family are trustworthy. Although I still have to have a talk with Eva, whom I haven't met yet, about her watery cooking.

Under the covers, my brothers have a gray blanket. If they sleep together, they should be warm. I tucked them in properly,

said

"Goodnight" to my younger brothers, and kissed their foreheads. Happiness!

"Don't put out the candle," the younger Henry pleaded, but "Winter is coming now. There will be many days when it's dark even during the day, so we must save," Nashius’s sensible words nearly made me, their big sister, cry.

"I'll come back to put it out. Go to sleep by then."

Truly, "Something must be done about this!" Oh dear, I wonder if my usual way of thinking is somehow becoming like Patience's.
