At the Advanced Dining Hall (Salon)

『Clang, clang』

Language class is over. This is a breeze for Patience, but it looks like I still need to study on my own. By the time I become a third-year student next year, I want to be able to skip a grade without relying on Patience! It's useless to try and escape reality like that, isn't it?

With heavy steps,

the Advanced Dining Hall

I head towards. I think about various things, like how this is my first time climbing these stairs, but the stares are painful.

"Patience, you're late."

Princess Margaret beckons me over, but isn't that the royal family's table? I thought I would be eating with Princess Margaret and her schoolmates, the daughters of high-ranking nobles.

Advanced Dining Hall

It's my first time here, so I didn't know that!

"I apologize for being late."

"Now, take a seat."

So this dining table has a tablecloth. The dining halls downstairs and in the dorms didn't have one. We lay out a tablecloth for dinner at home, too. While I was trying to escape reality to distract myself from the nervousness of sitting with Prince Richard and Prince Keith, the waiter presented a menu. There’s a menu, huh.

"I'll have the steak."

Prince Richard decides immediately. Prince Keith is also quick.

"Steak." Is he copying his older brother?

Princess Margaret is reading slowly. Even though it's a menu, it seems like you can only choose the main dish. What, there's fish! The first fish since I was reincarnated. I've decided on this, but I'll wait for Princess Margaret to decide. It wouldn't do for an attendant to choose first, right?

"I'll have the guinea fowl poêlé. What will you have, Patience?"

"I'll have the fish poêlé."

As I was handing the menu to the waiter, Prince Keith made a strange face.

"I can't believe someone actually eats fish."

Eh, is fish not delicious? I knew that since Romano is inland, fish is rarely eaten, but I didn't know it was considered bad. I feel disappointed, wondering if I made a mistake in choosing my menu today.

"Patience, is it for a memorial service or something?"

Princess Margaret also has a strange look on her face.

"No, I just haven't eaten fish in a while, so I wanted to try some."

I'm a little embarrassed. But even when I google Patience's memories, there's only a faint scene of her mother teaching her how to eat it.

The appetizer was a vegetable aspic. Very stylish, isn't it? But it seems Prince Keith hates vegetables too. He was scolded by Prince Richard for trying to leave it behind.

"Eat your vegetables, too. Mother told me to ensure you eat them because she's worried about your picky eating."

Ah, I see. I couldn't imagine Prince Keith's entourage warning him. It's left to Prince Richard and Princess Margaret.

The soup was consommé. Unlike at home, there are appetizers and main dishes, so it's fine for it to be without ingredients. I drink the soup with the elegant manners taught to me by Patience.

"Keith, learn from Patience."

Scolded by Prince Richard, Prince Keith sulks and makes a clattering sound with his spoon, prompting a warning from Princess Margaret. Please, don't drag me into this. He looks like he's saying "Hmph." If he keeps acting like that, he'll be intimidated by Prince Richard. It's a lunch filled with anxiety.

Prince Richard had a steak, Princess Margaret had guinea fowl poêlé, and in front of me was fish poêlé!

Ah, fish is such a hassle to eat properly. I’ll leave it to Patience and have a bite.

「Delicious!」I almost shed a tear. A month and a half after coming to this other world, it's my first time eating fish.

「Is it really that delicious?」

I answered Princess Margaret with a "Yes" and took a second bite. It really is delicious! The saltiness of the butter and the lemon are a perfect match.

「What a strange girl!」I can't hear any of Prince Keith's insults.

「Perhaps I shall have fish tomorrow.」

Princess Margaret seemed exasperated with me.

「That's true. When you eat it so deliciously, it makes me interested, too.」

Prince Richard, was I eating it that deliciously? I was trying to eat according to Patience's manners, at least.

「Brother, Sister, do not be deceived. Surely Patience would find anything she eats to be delicious.」

He was implicitly saying I’m a poor noble. But it’s the truth, so I don't mind. Prince Keith can't even imagine how poor I am. But if things go on like this, Prince Richard’s intimidation will start.

「Yes, that is correct. It was many times more delicious than the fish I have eaten before.」

I smiled and said to the servant who came to take the plates, "Please tell the chef that the fish was very delicious."

「It was that good? Then I shall order the fish tomorrow as well.」

Good grief, I'm glad Prince Richard’s attention was diverted. It’s my first time having sweets since coming to this other world, so I wish he’d stop with the intimidation next to me.

We get dessert at dinner back home, too. But I still can't afford sweets. I'd like to grow things like strawberries in a greenhouse once spring comes. Wait, maybe I can grow them if I have a greenhouse?

Feeling buoyant with anticipation, I quietly kept my mouth shut and waited. An attendant shouldn't be pushy, after all.

The dessert arrived before the other tables. A square block sat there, right in front of me.

The menu said "cake." Even though the appetizers, soup, and fish poêlé were so delicious... the sweets in this other world are in need of improvement!

『Sweet!』Since being reincarnated, I’ve been starving for sweets. And yet, this cake that tastes like a block of sugar is unacceptable. However, I did my best to finish it while drinking my tea. Patience is skin and bones, so I need to put on a little weight.

「I can't believe you can eat something that sweet.」

Oh, so this cake is too sweet even in this other world, huh? Would I be able to make money if I opened a sweets shop? But I don't have money to buy sugar or eggs, and I don't have any funds to open a store anywhere.

Not only Prince Keith but Prince Richard hasn't touched his either. Well, it is too sweet, but it's a waste.

「Do you want it?」

Honestly, I want to take it home for my younger siblings. If it's this sweet, it should last until Saturday. I almost blurted out "Yes," but I bit my tongue and held back. The Granger family is poor, but we are proud. If I’m going to bring home a souvenir, I’ll save my own portion to take back.
