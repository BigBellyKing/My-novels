Aoba Festival Music Club New Song Recital

I wake Princess Margaret up on the morning of the Aoba Festival too. I pray to Goddess Estena, wishing that one day she will wake up and get dressed on her own. After all, I prayed to Goddess Estena not to tell Queen Victoria that Prince Keith had gone out alone, and it came true. Since then, I’ve taken to praying every now and then. It feels similar to visiting a shrine for the New Year, I suppose.

Since the morning routine is becoming habitual, it’s getting faster.

"It is the Aoba Festival, so would you like to try a different hairstyle?"

My school friends who live at home have their hair curled or styled fluffily even on regular days. I imagine they’ll be wearing flashy decorations for the Aoba Festival.

"Let’s see, we have the new song recital too, so let’s add just a few decorations."

She took a hair accessory with a ribbon in the same green as her eyes out of the vanity and handed it to me.

"That’s a gorgeous ribbon accessory."

I usually just do a half-up style, but I try adding some braids like Mary does.

"Oh, you’re skillful, Patience. You could become a lady-in-waiting."

Becoming a lady-in-waiting is one of my future career options, so I’ll take that as a compliment. Though, I wouldn't want to be the one waking Princess Margaret up forever.

Our Music Club’s performance is first. After that, it’s the Chorus Club, then the second Music Club performance. Following the lunch break, there’s the Drama Club, the Chorus Club, the third Music Club performance, and the Chorus Club. I’d like to see the play. After all, there’s no TV or radio in this other world.

"What shall we do today?"

We usually spend meals and after-school hours together, but we go our separate ways during class. That’s because Princess Margaret takes classes with her school friends, but I wonder what will happen during the Aoba Festival. I ask for her instructions.

"Since we are performing first, we will head to the auditorium after breakfast. Catherine and the others will probably be watching the Knight Club matches in the morning. You may listen to the chorus, and I’d like to hear the second Music Club performance as well."

In other words, since my school friends won’t be around in the morning, it looks like I have to stay by her side the whole time.

"In the afternoon, you may feel free to go see other club presentations."

Princess Margaret really loves music, so she will surely not leave the auditorium all day long. I want to see the play, but I also want to observe other club presentations a little. There was an 'Alchemy Club' in the pamphlet, so I want to see what that’s like. After all, there was no alchemy in my previous life. I’m extremely interested.

A harpsichord had been placed on the auditorium stage. Albert was giving various instructions to the academy servants. The repertoire stand had the songs to be performed written on it. President Melissa turns the pages for me, but afterwards, it’s my turn as the underclassman.

"Let’s leave that side to them."

Princess Margaret sat in the back with President Melissa to keep her distance from the noisy Albert. As her personal attendant, I sat in the seat behind Princess Margaret, too.

"Albert is really enthusiastic, isn't he? He’s always strange, but today he’s at a dangerous level."

I nearly burst out laughing at President Melissa’s harsh words. He’s at maximum tension, after all. Just looking at him is exhausting.

Clang, clang

The bell for the start of the first-period class rang. In other words, the Aoba Festival is beginning. Students and parents are trickling in to fill the seats in the auditorium.

We also moved backstage. I’m starting to feel a bit nervous. Since we are second-years, we play first. Some club members complained that people wouldn’t gather because it overlaps with the Knight Club, but personally, I think plenty of people have gathered.

“Patience, after my greeting ends, go out onto the stage.”

President Melissa gave a truly dignified greeting and flipped the first page of the program placed on the stage left side.

Amidst the applause, I walked out onto the stage, managed to bow gracefully, and then played Hanon. I have loved “Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star Variations” since my previous life. I love the feeling of notes flying around midway through, as if stars are truly twinkling.

“Minute Waltz” is also a cute piece, isn’t it?

“Träumerei” is a song that was played for me during nap time when I was little, and it makes me feel nostalgic.

I managed to finish playing. I stood up from the chair and bowed.

“Wonderful!” Even amidst the sound of applause, there was a very resounding voice. One parent stood up and looks excited, but I flipped the program and headed backstage.

“My, the applause won’t stop. What a dilemma.”

President Melissa looks happy about the good reception. Not only is there applause, but there are also shouts of “Encore.” That very resounding voice from earlier is likely from that parent. They are wearing flashy clothes, so perhaps they are a high-ranking noble?

“Naturally. That is because they are all wonderful new pieces. However, my new piece is also wonderful.”

If it were by grade, it would be third-year Princess Margaret, but even though the official stance is that it has nothing to do with social standing, fourth-year Albert plays first. The new piece was a very technical one. It reminded me of the “Transcendental Études” from my previous life. After the intense piece, “Eine kleine Nachtmusik” felt heartfelt.

Amidst the applause, I walked out onto the stage to flip the program. It’s a bit embarrassing. Next up is Princess Margaret. The new piece was refreshing and fitting for the Aoba Festival. The “Turkish March” was played with great energy. And since the practice for the “Minuet” was also solid, it received great applause.

Lastly, I flip the program. As expected, the finale is President Melissa. The president’s new piece has a grand feeling to it. It felt liberating. The subsequent performance of “Für Elise” by Catherine surprisingly highlighted President Melissa’s maiden-like qualities.

“This concludes the first new music recital of the Music Club. The second new music recital will be after the Chorus Club. We will be announcing other new pieces as well, so please do listen.”

With a round of applause that seemed to shake the auditorium, the first new music recital of the Music Club came to an end. My turn is over, so I feel relieved. Let’s enjoy the Aoba Festival!
