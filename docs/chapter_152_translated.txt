The Nervous Aoba Festival

The Music Club’s new song recital is sandwiched between the Chorus Club’s performances. I’m not looking forward to seeing Louise, but since we’re sandwiched in, it feels inevitable that I’ll run into her.

“As usual, the Chorus Club’s songs are so old-fashioned.”

Princess Margaret’s assessment is harsh. Louise is singing too, but it’s all schoolgirls, so it feels lacking in impact.

“I believe there were supposed to be male students as well?”

There were several male students at the Autumn Harvest Festival, but I don’t see any here. Did they move to the afternoon session?

“My, Patience, you’re behind on the news. The male students have moved to the Glee Club. I heard quite a few female students have transferred over, too. In the Chorus Club, unless you’re a high-ranking noble, you aren’t allowed to sing a solo. It’s not a meritocracy, so the students who were dissatisfied moved.”

I see, that’s why I felt the chorus lacked depth. The number of people has decreased, too. I was thinking such carefree thoughts, but next is the Music Club’s new song recital. I place the Hanon, which had been pushed to the side of the stage, in the center and set up the music stand.

Club President Albert greeted everyone briefly, as if used to it: “We will now begin the Music Club’s new song recital.”

There are many first-years this year, so I don’t have to do the page-turning, which is a relief. It seems the first-years decided their order by drawing lots. First is Klaus. Seriously, he looks like an angel, my type exactly! He played my "Kitten Waltz" and a new piece that rearranged "Weeds" into a "Flower Garden."

“Bravo!” Oh, Duke Laforet is making a scene again this year.

Next is Samuel. His new song is "Spring Lightning"—it’s a bit rock-like, and I like it. He played "Nocturne." Yeah, Duke Laforet is excited and making a scene. Club President Albert went to stop him. Thank goodness.

Daniel’s new song was the hardest to arrange, but we managed to make it look decent. He played "Farewell," and he’s skilled.

Bardiche’s new song is "Trot." It’s a dance tune that captures the feeling of a horse’s trot. He also played one of Club President Albert’s transcendent virtuoso pieces. His fingers move incredibly well!

I played "Heroic Polonaise" and Princess Margaret’s "Song of Youth."

Yes, it ended with a round of applause; I’m glad. Next up is the Chorus Club’s performance.

“Patience, that was wonderful.”

It was nice to be praised by Princess Margaret, but is that shadow approaching us Duke Laforet?

“Patience Granger, was it? A wonderful talent. Would you like to come to my estate?”

Club President Albert stepped in, but his eyes were so serious it was scary.

“Father, the recital is still ongoing, so let’s take our seats.”

Princess Margaret also looks worried.

“Duke Laforet is quite dangerous. Patience, you must be careful. Is there anyone you can get engaged to?”

A marriage proposal for the poor Granger family... come to think of it, Aunt Monteraside brought one up over winter break. I declined immediately, though.

I don’t want to be Club President Albert’s stepmother. If it comes down to it, I might have to escape into that engagement. I hope he’s not too old. I have a shota complex, so older gentlemen are a no-go. I was pondering this, and realized the Chorus Club’s performance was boring enough to let me think. Louise is performing again. The number of people really has decreased.

“Now, let’s head to the back.”

Since the Glee Club’s performance is next, we, the accompanists, head to the dressing room. It was bustling with members of the Glee Club wearing glamorous costumes. Members of the Dance Club are also participating as backup dancers, so it’s overflowing with people.

It’s a massive difference from the Chorus Club’s performance with their few members.

I’m on the Hanon, Princess Margaret is on the flute, Bardiche, Samuel, and Daniel are on the lute, and Rupert and Klaus are on percussion. Club President Albert is conducting.

"Alec and Eliza" was very good. The audience is also delighted and giving a standing ovation. In particular, the part where Eliza sings "Amazing Grace" while praying to God for the safety of Alec, who went to the battlefield, was a masterpiece. I can certainly see why Louise would want to sing it.

“The Music Club might not be able to get first place with this. In any case, let’s have lunch.”

“Patience, I intend to persuade my father during lunch, but make sure you do not leave Princess Margaret’s side. My father is the type to be more obsessed with music than even me. I don’t know what he might do.”

I was warned by Club President Albert to stay by Princess Margaret’s side. It seems his own son is worried that Duke Laforet might kidnap me. He really is a dangerous character.

Since I’m in the Alchemy Club in the afternoon, I cast one vote for the "Music Club."

The Upper Dining Hall

was partitioned off just like the previous year. If parents come to greet Princess Margaret or Prince Keith, they won’t be able to eat in peace. I have lunch with Prince Keith, Ralph, and Hugo, as usual.

“Sister Margaret, what will you be doing this afternoon?”

The Knights Club matches end in the morning. I’m going to the Alchemy Club, so I had hoped Prince Keith would stay by Princess Margaret’s side, but the conversation moves in a different direction.

“I intend to sample ice cream at the Alchemy Club after lunch. After that, it’s the new song recital in the auditorium. So, I would like to ask Keith to accompany Patience. I want you to keep an eye on her so Duke Laforet doesn’t take her home.”

“Surely, he wouldn’t go that far. It’ll be fine.”

I hurriedly declined. Being with Prince Keith makes me feel tense.

“Duke Laforet is single, isn’t he? If he’s a music-obsessed fool like Albert, it’s dangerous.”

Is it really okay for a Duke in this country to be such an eccentric?

“Exactly. Just a while ago, he told Patience that she should definitely come to his estate. Albert blocked him, but if I’m not there, she can’t defy him.”

Prince Keith’s eyebrows raise. He looks angry. Indeed, it’s not right for a middle-aged father to make unreasonable demands of an 11-year-old girl. And since he’s a Duke, I can’t defy him.

“I understand. I will not leave Patience’s side.”

It was decided that he would walk me to the sewing room at the end of the fourth period. And once the fourth period is over, the parents will go home from the academy, and from then on, I will act alongside Princess Margaret.

“Don’t you have places you want to see as well, Prince Keith? I’ll be at the Alchemy Club from here on, so I’ll be fine.”

I tried to decline, but neither Princess Margaret nor Prince Keith would let me.

“Patience, you don’t know how scary nobles can be yet. If you were dragged off to his estate, even against your will, bad rumors would spread, and you wouldn't be able to have a decent marriage. Becoming Duke Laforet’s second wife would be the best-case scenario; you might end up being treated as a mistress.”

A middle-aged man’s mistress! I got goosebumps. No way!

I declined dessert since I’d be sampling ice cream, and we decided to head to the Alchemy Club’s exhibition hall.
