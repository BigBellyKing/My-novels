A needle threader is incredibly convenient!

Friday afternoon is the Alchemy Club. But before that, I need to water the advanced medicinal herbs and antidote herbs in the greenhouse with purified water and cast spells on them as well. Since I have to cast spells only on the buds of the antidote herb leaves, it takes a little time.

"Today, let's make a needle threader! After that, I hope we can make parts for a bicycle. I wish there was something I could use as a substitute for the tires and brake rubber."

I quickly cast a daily life magic spell on myself and hurry to the Alchemy Club. I'll make sure not to be late today, of all days.

"Oh, Patience, you're late."

I get told I'm late every time, but it’s only the third period.

"Now, let's build a bicycle!"

Caesar, the club president, is full of motivation, but I want to make a needle threader. Besides, I think this is a product that could sell well at Barnes Trading Company. Well, I don't know what kind of things Barnes Trading Company sells, though.

"President Caesar, today I want to make a needle threader."

I show him the diagram, but he looks overtly disappointed.

"Instead of something like this, it should be a bicycle! Come on, I've already made the blueprints for the parts. All that’s left is to actually make them and assemble them."

The bicycle blueprints were drawn very precisely.

"Excuse me, President Caesar, are there materials for these tires and brakes? I want to put air inside them. I need a material that is elastic and durable."

President Caesar, who had been leaning forward, ready to get started, suddenly stops.

"You want to put air inside? Hmm, elastic and durable? How about slime?"

Benjamin also arrives, and they start discussing the tire material. I take the opportunity to make a needle threader.

"This thinness won't pass through the eye of a needle. It might be fine for an embroidery needle. I have to make it even thinner!"

I failed a few times, but I made two types of needle threaders. A slightly thicker but sturdy one for embroidery needles, and an ultra-fine wire needle threader for regular needles. After making them for Princess Margaret, myself, and Mary, I think for a moment and make ten of each. They are samples for Barnes Trading Company, and trial products to give to my embroidery teacher and sewing teacher.

"Patience, let's put the tires and brakes on hold and make other parts. Carriage brakes are made of metal, so that should be fine for now. Let's try making the tires and the like out of wood or metal too."

President Caesar seems to want to assemble the bicycle no matter what. Since I’ve finished the needle threader, I’ll help him make it.

"This chain is difficult. Patience, do you want to try making it?"

I look closely at the blueprint Caesar drew, and while recalling the image of a chain from my previous life, I chant, "Make a chain!"

"This looks pretty good. Let's make the other parts too!"

Other members arrive, and we take turns making them. Since there are so many parts, I think I’d run out of magic power if I made them all by myself. The sizes are written properly, so it should be fine if they are made correctly.

"Now, let's assemble it!"

Personally, I don't like the metal wheels, but even the Alchemy Club doesn't have slime powder in stock. The boys assemble it with glee. There were a few defects in some parts, but we remade them and the first bicycle was finished.

"Is this a bicycle?"

Since Bryce and Arthur had been participating since the part-making stage, they didn't seem to fully understand the overall picture.

"I think it’s better if the wheels aren't made of metal, but it’s complete for now."

Everyone’s eyes turn to me. What?

"So, Patience, how do you make this run?"

Ah, right! Nobody has ever ridden a bicycle before.

"It's my first time too, but I'll try riding it."

As I straddle the frame and sit on the saddle, Bryce stops me, saying, "Wait a minute!"

"Patience, is that way of riding it okay?"

For a moment, I didn't understand what he meant, but I realize that this is a world where women aren't supposed to straddle horses either. But unlike a horse, the frame is thin, so it should be fine, right?

"Yes, I can't imagine any other way to ride it."

For some reason, Bryce is blushing. But the problem is whether I can ride it or not.

I take hold of the handlebars, put my feet on the pedals, and turn them. Yeah, the ride quality is bad because the wheels are metal. On top of that, the saddle is metal too. I want to put leather with cushion inside it.

I ride around inside the clubhouse. Yep, the brakes need improvement too!

"Amazing! Let me ride it too!"

President Caesar and the club members are like children. But, practice is needed. I can only move by pushing off with my feet.

"As expected, I want to make the tires with air inside. The brakes don't work well either. Also, I must make the saddle leather to improve the comfort."

They aren't listening to me. Everyone is obsessed with riding the bicycle.

"Patience, this is amazing! You can travel fast even without a horse. It's a groundbreaking invention!"

Bicycles are convenient. Well, you have to pedal yourself, so you can't go far. But I think it's enough within the Royal Capital of Romano.

"President Caesar, let's make the tires. Also, we have to improve the brakes and the saddle."

He wasn't listening before, but it seems it finally reached his ears.

"I see, in that case, we need a bell."

Benjamin is already thinking about making a bell. It seems bells exist in this other world, too. There seems to be a system for calling servants where pulling a string in a room rings a bell in the servant's room. The Granger estate is large, but it isn't fully equipped with such a system. The Duke Laforet family estate, which I stopped by on the way to the summer villa, was so huge that if they didn't have one, I doubt a handbell would be heard.

"President Caesar, could you show these needle threaders to your father? This ultra-fine one is for regular needles, and the slightly thicker one is for embroidery thread."

President Caesar looked at the needle threader with a puzzled expression, but when I wrapped it in paper and handed it to him, he said he would "show them."

He doesn't seem to quite understand the convenience of the needle threader.

"I'm worried whether your father can understand the convenience of these needle threaders. Please tell him to have the estate’s housekeeper or a maid use them."

Maybe men can't understand the convenience of this? Ah, men like tailors are the exception, though.

"I have to go back to the dormitory!"

It’s starting to get dark. Today, since Bryce is also obsessed with the bicycle, I return to the dormitory alone.

"Tomorrow is the study session, and the day after is the Duke of Barnes' estate. My time with my younger brothers will decrease."

This time, I managed to reach the dormitory before it got dark. Princess Margaret is at the palace, so it's free time. I'll do my embroidery side work and study administration and law. Ah, and I also have to read 'Musician Berière' and write a report. I've been reading it bit by bit, but this is more of a household account book than a diary. I don't have enough concentration, so I haven't finished reading it. Yeah, I'll give up on finishing it, summarize the income and expenses from the time they were in the Lawrence Kingdom, write out the income and expenses after going to the Estena Holy Empire, and compare them. It's obvious at a glance. Besides, I finished the report with excerpts from the diary.

I felt I couldn't quite keep up with Duke Laforet's love for music, but perhaps musicians wouldn't stay in the Lawrence Kingdom without a patron like that. As far as I studied in administration, I didn't see any departments for cultural project support. It feels like music-loving nobles, theater-loving nobles, and literature-loving nobles are providing aid as patrons. Yeah, I’ll add the necessity of an organization that supports cultural projects to the report.

After finishing the report, I eat slowly in the dining room downstairs. Yeah, this sense of relaxation is nice. Now, side work, side work!

I am learning pictorial embroidery in Embroidery 3. Yes, since I’m stitching with fine silk thread, it’s not progressing much. I’m learning the method little by little so as not to succumb to the temptation of wanting to use daily life magic. Once I learn the method, maybe it would be okay to use daily life magic. Otherwise, it will take a year.

Right now, I’m embroidering the bridal trousseau for a noble lady I don't know. It’s surprising that even napkins like this need one's own and the partner's initials embroidered on them. It’s tough. Well, it’s fine since I’m getting paid for it. Since this is side work, I’ll use daily life magic from the start to get through the numbers. Taking this many napkins means she’s probably rich enough to hold dinner parties and balls.

"That's it for the embroidery."

Tomorrow morning, I’ll go to the greenhouse, and after that, I just have to wait for Mary to come. I hope she comes soon. I will definitely eat lunch at home with my younger brothers! Since it’s the second study session, my aunt should be a little calmer.
