The Viscount Granger Family

Pale winter sunlight shone onto my face through the old, thin curtains. It seems Patience’s habits prevailed, as I was sleeping with my arms out over the thin comforter, fingers elegantly clasped.

“Ugh… it’s cold.”

I tried to burrow into the covers, but my stomach let out a loud rumble.

“I can’t do anything if I don’t eat breakfast.”

Even though my mind says “I’m starving,” the words that come out of my mouth are translated into “Patience.” Even “Mom” becomes “Mother.” I don’t know if that’s convenient or inconvenient.

Searching my memories, the only maid here is Mary, who has red hair. Beyond that, there is Eva the cook, George the footman, and Wyatt the butler, who manages everything.

“Are nobles really like this?”

I had an image of there being many more maids, but that’s because the Granger family has issues.

Patience only knows this lifestyle, but apparently, her father, Viscount Granger, is a member of the robe nobility and holds no fief.

Normally, he would work at the royal palace and receive a salary, but it seems he was dismissed. I think this is incredibly serious, but Patience’s memories don’t have the details. The young lady doesn’t seem to have any interest in salaries. However, she does remember that the number of maids began to decrease a few years before her mother passed away.

“In other words, no pay! And it seems like this has been going on for about four years?”

Patience’s younger brother, Henry, is six. That means her mother passed away leaving behind a cute little son of about four years old. Well, at that time, Nasius was six and Patience was eight.

“Well, at ten years old, I suppose I wouldn’t understand my father’s unemployment.”

That’s right, I’m ten! Whether you call it being rejuvenated or just becoming a child, well, it’s better than being an old woman.

It’s obvious that no maid is coming to help me dress, no matter how long I wait, so I get out of bed.

“So, I wash my face with this.”

On the sideboard, there was a pitcher inside a porcelain washbasin that still had the faint remnants of what must have once been a cute floral pattern.

“The toothbrush is… don’t tell me, this coarse cloth…”

I have memories of toothbrushes, but it seems that right now, I’m just supposed to scrub my teeth with this coarse cloth. This needs to be improved immediately!

After finishing washing my face with the lukewarm water, I try opening the dresser. There are two dresses, but assuming the lace one is for summer, I choose a long-sleeved, thin, light blue one.

“Isn’t this… not meant for winter?”

It is long-sleeved, at least, but it’s paper-thin. Mary’s maid uniform seems warmer than this, though that’s cotton while this appears to be silk. Are they trying to maintain the appearance of nobility? Or maybe it’s a hand-me-down. I’m pretty sure there were relatives.

“Let’s see, the undergarments are… drawers and a chemise… and the socks are cotton, I see.”

I secure my socks with garters and put on the paper-thin, supposedly silk dress.

“It’s cold!”

I look for something to wear over it, but there is only a coat in the dresser.

“Is it not allowed to wear a coat?”

Patience’s memory tells me “No, you can’t!” causing me to pull back the hand I had reached toward the coat.

“There should be a fireplace in the dining room.”

Even staying in a cold room won't fill my stomach. I tried to head to the dining room quickly, but was interrupted by Patience.

『You have to make the bed! Fold the nightclothes and put them in the chest, too.』

I wanted to grumble that, in reality, that should be a maid's job, but Mary is the only one here. Surely Mary is

busy taking care of

my younger brothers.

Since Patience is an impoverished noble, she was used to making beds. Besides, the duvet is thin, so it’s easy.

Permission was finally granted, so I hurried toward the dining room. I don't get lost because I have Patience's memories of this area. Despite being poor, the mansion seems to be large; I went down the gently spiraling staircase to the dining room on the first floor.

「Good morning.」

My father, William, was reading a newspaper at the dining table. Since there was also a small fire in the fireplace, it was warmer than the bedroom.

「Patience, good morning. Are you already feeling better?」

William has brown hair mixed with a little gray, and pale gray eyes. His eyes, lifted from the newspaper, look worried.

「Yes, Father. I'm sorry for causing you worry.」

I feel a fondness for my refined-looking father, but I can't expect any survival skills from him at all. William, while perhaps not to the extent of Patience, is also skin and bones, and the sleeves of his jacket are quite frayed. My chest aches—is it from Patience's memories?—at the thought that I want to dress him in a warmer jacket.

「Father, sister, good morning.」

My younger brothers arrived in the dining room, led by Mary. Seriously, angels!

「Nashius, Henry! Good morning.」

「Sister, are you really okay now?」

The older one, Nashius, has brown hair and gray eyes, looking exactly like our father. I’d rather he didn't inherit the lack of survival skills.

「Yes, Nashius. Thank you.」

I kiss Nashius on the cheek. Seriously, I'm so happy! Judging by the fact that Patience doesn't stop me, it seems to be a daily morning routine.

「Then, sister, can we play?」

The younger one, Henry, has blonde hair and blue eyes, just like me. He took after our mother. I must make sure he doesn't die young.

「Yes, let's play.」

I lift Henry up and kiss his cheek. Ah, bliss!!

This sense of happiness did not last long.

『Gross!』

A soup with a few thinly-flavored vegetables floating in it, and a piece of bread that is hard even when thinly sliced. And there's only one slice at that. A diet is definitely not necessary for the skin-and-bones Patience.

Besides, to think that I can only feed my younger brothers a breakfast like this! Even though they are at an age where they eat a lot, it's terrible.

But, with the refinement instilled by Patience, I drink the soup slowly. Although there isn't really any flavor to savor. I have to investigate whether the cook, Eva, has poor skills, or if the household finances are so tight that we can only make food like this.
