Summer Palace: Let's get someone else to take over the morning wake-up call

In the morning, after Mary helped me get ready, I went to wake up Princess Margaret. Last night, Queen Victoria told me, "Patience, don't let Margaret be late for breakfast." Good grief.

I had already told Princess Margaret's lady-in-waiting, Sophie, to prepare tea last night.

"Princess Margaret, please wake up."

Sophie and Lady-in-waiting Charlotte are trying to wake her up together, but Princess Margaret is fast asleep.

"Please wake up." I used life magic to stimulate her blood circulation while waking her.

"Good morning." I handed the tea Sophie had brewed to Princess Margaret, who had finally woken up.

"As expected, Patience, you're good at waking people up. I wonder if there's any other lady-in-waiting who is good at life magic?"

I cast life magic on Princess Margaret after she drank her tea, saying, "Become beautiful!" I think it will be fine to leave it to Sophie from here on.

"I'll head to the dining room first."

As I left the room, I was caught by Lady-in-waiting Charlotte.

"Lady Patience, how did you wake Princess Margaret?"

"It's life magic. That's the only kind I can use."

It's a slightly strange kind of life magic, though. Oh, but I want to quit being Princess Margaret's wake-up attendant. Lady-in-waiting Charlotte also seems quite desperate. I suppose she's been having a hard time waking her up on weekends.

"I can use life magic too, but I can't do anything like that. Please, teach me."

I want Lady-in-waiting Charlotte to do her best to wake her up.

"Professor Jefferson said that life magic can do anything. So, Lady Charlotte, if you do your best, you can do it too. I think Princess Margaret is bad at waking up in the morning because she has low blood pressure."

I wonder if low blood pressure doesn't exist in this other world? Ah, Lady-in-waiting Charlotte doesn't seem to understand. Her eyes are darting around.

"Um, it's similar to how women who suffer from poor circulation have cold hands and feet on cold days and find it hard to move them. It's hard to wake up because the blood flow gets worse while sleeping. So, I cast life magic to improve that blood flow."

Lady-in-waiting Charlotte is still not understanding, saying, "Improve blood flow?"

"You can improve blood circulation without using life magic. Massaging the legs with a hot, steamed towel would also encourage her to wake up."

Finally, Lady-in-waiting Charlotte's face brightens.

"Ah, I can prepare that. So I should massage her legs. Maids often do that for young ladies who are tired from dancing at balls, so Sophie should be used to it too."

Please, do your best! If this means she can wake up, I'll be free from the duty of being Princess Margaret's wake-up attendant.

After breakfast, while I was relaxing in the salon, the nanny brought in Princess Jane and Prince Marcus. Ah, so cute. Princess Jane is a little Princess Margaret. And Prince Marcus is a little Prince Keith.

"Good morning."

I greeted the Queen and politely received kisses in turn.

"Jane, Marcus, this is Patience, Margaret's lady-in-waiting. You'll be together at the Summer Palace, so get along well."

Ugh, Princess Jane and I are about the same height. I'm just a little taller, aren't I?

"Princess Jane, Prince Marcus, and I am Patience."

Huh, so that's all it takes for the nanny to take them to the nursery? This is the first time I've been glad the Granger family is poor. No, I don't actually want to be poor. It's just too lonely to only be able to see my younger brothers for such a fleeting moment.

"Margaret, Keith, I think you should study this morning. Margaret, math and needlework; Keith, the classics."

That's strict for summer vacation. Just as I was watching from the sidelines like it had nothing to do with me, the sparks flew my way.

"Patience must also learn the lute, dancing, horseback riding, and swimming."

My plan to accompany Princess Margaret was stalled by Prince Richard.

"Leave that for later. Patience, about the matter we discussed yesterday, I'd like to try it out right away."

Oh, was I saved? Ah, but Queen Victoria seems a bit skeptical.

"Richard, what are you planning to do? Patience is Margaret's lady-in-waiting."

Even Prince Richard, who will be the next king, seems weak when it comes to the Queen. It looks like he has to follow proper protocol.

"Margaret, I want to try and see if I can make salt from seawater with Patience. Could you lend Patience to me for a bit?"

It seems Princess Margaret decided to do Prince Richard a favor.

"Very well, I will lend Patience to you, brother, while I am studying. But, she has lute practice from the afternoon, mind you."

The Queen also nodded, "Very well." Good grief, I wonder which will be harder: the lute practice or making salt?
