Alchemy Club

I hadn't been told to go to the Royal Palace by Thursday. In other words, I'm free from the fourth period on Friday.

Benjamin says with a smile as he leaves homeroom, "I'll be waiting for you in the fourth period."

"Lady Patience, have you made some sort of promise to Benjamin?"

Phillips, who was walking with me to the Law and Economics classroom, asks me.

"Yes, I joined the Alchemy Club, so I'm going for the first time today."

Phillips' face stiffened. I guess that's how people react to the Alchemy Club.

He said, "Is that so," but perhaps I won't be treated like a 'young lady' anymore because of this. Well, that’s fine, as it won't make me feel awkward anymore.

But for some reason, the "young lady" address continued as usual. It’s a mystery.

For lunch, I ate with the usual members at the

Advanced Cafeteria.

で食べる。

"You didn't come yesterday."

Prince Keith says something unnecessary.

"Well, Patience. Where did you eat? Surely you aren't on a diet?"

There's no need for a diet with this skinny body.

"No, I just thought I'd like to eat leisurely at the lower cafeteria sometimes."

Princess Margaret warned me, saying, "Even if I'm not there, you must use the

Advanced Cafeteria."

I thought this topic was over, but Prince Keith seemed to have doubts.

"Did you eat alone, Patience?"

Ah, please don't ask me such hard-to-answer questions.

"Before I became a lady-in-waiting, I used to eat alone at the cafeteria." It's not a lie, but I'm being evasive.

"Patience, who did you eat with?"

Princess Margaret saw right through me that I was hiding something.

"With Benjamin-sama and Caesar-sama."

Princess Margaret's eyebrows rise slightly. She's in "angry mode."

"Patience, you're so careless, that's why you get invited to things like the Alchemy Club. That is why I told you to eat at the

Advanced Cafeteria."

で食べなさいと言っているでしょ」

That may be so, but unless we are together, I feel bad about having someone else pay for my meals when I am alone, and it makes me feel heavy-hearted. It’s like eating without paying. Let's talk about this later.

"Did you really join the Alchemy Club?"

Prince Keith is about to get caught by my "gaffe radar" again.

"Yes, I was told it's fine to go just two or three times a month."

Prince Keith is frowning unhappily. Why? It has nothing to do with him.

"Did Caesar-sama and Benjamin-sama eat at the student cafeteria?"

Hugo seems shocked. This boy is a pampered child, after all.

"Why at the cafeteria?" Even the usually calm Ralph is confused.

"Did you invite them to the cafeteria, Patience?"

Do I really need to answer Prince Keith's interrogation?

"No, Benjamin-sama told me to try talking to Caesar-sama once, and since Princess Margaret was taking cooking class on Thursday, that's just how it happened."

Princess Margaret frowned, perhaps remembering the terrible lunch.

"I'm having a really hard time because you received your certificate, Patience. I had to eat potatoes that were crunchy in the middle. If you had been there, you would have cooked them through properly."

As expected, even Prince Keith and the others didn't say it was my fault. Obviously.

"Margaret-sama, did you take notes on Mr. Spencer's explanation properly?"

"I did. But the inside was still crunchy."

That's strange. Mr. Spencer is very careful to ensure even complete beginners don't fail.

"By any chance, didn't you check if they were done by piercing them with a skewer?"

Princess Margaret’s eyes go wide with surprise.

"My, Patience. Can you use clairvoyance? Mr. Spencer pointed that out to me later."

"No, I can't use clairvoyance. I just imagined it."

Prince Keith and the others were also stunned, and seemed to have forgotten about the cafeteria incident. Thank goodness. The fact that I thought that shows how foolish I am.

"From next week, let's eat together on Thursdays. I don't know what you'll do if I take my eyes off you, Patience."

Eh, I want to avoid that. I am Princess Margaret's lady-in-waiting, not Prince Keith's. Before I can refuse, Princess Margaret smiles and agrees.

"That's right, I'd feel relieved if we did that. This child has absolutely no self-awareness. Joining the Alchemy Club is like a rabbit jumping into a wolf's den. Well, since Bryce is joining too, I'm slightly relieved. If Benjamin or Caesar ask you to do anything unreasonable, ask Bryce for help. For someone taking the wizard course, Bryce is still the most sensible one."

Prince Keith's eyes are scary. Is he angry about something? Could he still be thinking I joined the Alchemy Club just to get close to Caesar? That's not it, but I don't need to make excuses, right?

Etiquette 2 was "Have a luncheon party hosted for you in the spring semester." Yeah, surely Etiquette 3 is a dinner party. But for a luncheon, I have to think about the menu too. I don't remember ever holding a luncheon at the poor Granger house. But I was served feasts every day at the summer palace. I'll write it down based on that.

"Patience, you can host a wonderful luncheon. You pass. While you're at it, write up a plan for the dinner party in Etiquette 3 as well."

Oh, maybe I'll get my certificate. I'll do my best! I used the summer palace dinners as a reference for this too.

"I'll give you your certificate."

Yes, at this rate, Princess Margaret will get her certificate soon too. I'll tell her later.

Princess Margaret went back to the Royal Palace after Sophie came to pick her up. Which means it's time to go to the Alchemy Club.

"Good day," I greet them as a formality, but it’s a greeting that doesn't fit at all, isn't it?

"Oh, Patience, you're late. Let me introduce everyone."

Department Head Caesar says it’s an introduction, but there are only Benjamin, Bryce, and one other person there.

"This is fifth-year Arthur Miller. And this is fourth-year Patience Granger."

"It is a pleasure to meet you," I greet them, but I involuntarily take a step back at how filthy the lab coats everyone except Bryce is wearing are.

"About those lab coats..." I want to clean them with life magic, but maybe there's a reason they're kept dirty.

"Oh, that's right. Bryce and Patience, you should wear lab coats too. We handle hot metal and glass, and use chemicals, so your parents will scold you if your uniforms get covered in holes."

He offers us the seniors' spares, but I refuse to wear these.

"Department Head Caesar, is it alright if I clean these?"

"It's fine, but they'll get dirty again immediately."

I don't care if they get dirty immediately; I hate wearing such dirty lab coats.

I chanted "Become clean!" and everyone's lab coats turned snow-white. This is what a lab coat should look like. The charred holes were also sealed up.

"As expected, Patience's life magic is unique. So, is there anything you and Bryce want to make?"

There are plenty of things I want to make. But first is a magic lamp for my younger brothers' room.

"Department Head Caesar, couldn't we adjust the brightness of the magic lamp with a switch? I want to be able to adjust it for when it's bright, slightly bright, and when I want to turn it off."

Caesar crosses his arms and thinks.

"Hmm, it's possible. But what's the point? Isn't it enough to just be able to turn a magic lamp on and off?"

Benjamin, Bryce, and Arthur are also tilting their heads.

"My younger brother is scared of it being pitch black before he sleeps. So, I thought it wouldn't be as scary if there was a dim light."

I wondered if the four boys wouldn't understand, but it seems their memories of their own childhood rooms were revived.

"I see, that's a good idea. To make that, we have to change the mechanism of the switch."

From then on, Department Head Caesar and I started thinking about the switch adjustment.

"Actually, rather than a switch, I'd like to use a knob that you can turn to adjust the brightness."

I roughly drew a semi-circular dome-shaped cover and the base, and added a knob to the center of the base.

"Adjusting the brightness with this knob? Yeah, it looks like that could work."

Department Head Caesar quickly draws out a mechanism where turning the knob gradually moves the magic stone closer to the magic circle.

If I can make a bedside lamp that can adjust brightness like in my previous life, Henry won't have to be scared before going to sleep.

While Department Head Caesar and I were engrossed in this, Bryce was making a magic lamp with Benjamin.

"Your way of drawing the magic circle is all wrong."

He's getting scolded by his senior, Arthur. Oh, Benjamin is being scolded for being sloppy too.

"Hey, Patience, is now the time to be looking away? Let's try this knob. Memorize the blueprint and make it yourself."

Since it's quite different from the switch we made in class, I seriously memorize the blueprint.

"Alright, give it a try."

Department Head Caesar is surprisingly spartan. Metal was melting in the kiln. I pictured the knob I had memorized in my head and chanted, "Make the knob."

The melted metal took the shape in my head and fell to the side with a clunk.

"Not bad. But we won't know if this will successfully adjust the brightness until we test it."

Since the magic circle is already drawn, we conduct the experiment.

"First of all, if it doesn't light up, it's a non-starter."

I turned the knob, and the light came on.

"Try turning the knob the other way a little at a time."

When I turned it gently, it got a little darker.

"I think it's easier to sleep when it's a little darker."

"Then try turning it a little more."

When I turned it a little more, it went out.

"Hmm? The light turns on when the magic stone and the magic circle touch, and goes out when they are separated. It's difficult to keep a dim light in between. Is it just a matter of adjusting the knob?"

In my previous life, the bedside lamp could adjust its brightness by turning a knob. Why was that? Something must have been obstructing the flow of electricity to the light. But since that was just a common object, I don't remember how it worked.

"Is there no magic circle for light that can get brighter or darker?"

Department Head Caesar looks at me in surprise.

"Patience, that's a wild request. But, it's interesting. A challenge for a new magic circle! We're staying up all night tonight."

Oh my, it's completely pitch black outside the window.

"Patience, I'll take you to the dormitory."

As expected, Bryce was a reasonable person. The other members were in the middle of a heated debate.

"Everyone, are you really going to stay up all night?"

Wouldn't their parents worry if they didn't go home? Oh, are the carriages coming to pick them up?

"It's nothing for you to worry about, Patience. Besides, the clubhouse has a set closing time, so the carriages should come to pick them up then."

I thanked Bryce for walking me to the dormitory, but I feel like if I'm not careful, I might get sucked too deep into the Alchemy Club.
