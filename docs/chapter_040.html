Home

I went out to the city.

After finishing a tiring lunch, Princess Margaret had an art class. I already received my certificate in art, so I am exempt. It’s time to head out to the city.

Still, the guard in front of the school gate looks scary. Since this is an academy where royals and nobles study, strict security is only natural. If I act composed, it should be fine.

"Right, I've never walked through the gate before."

It was always by carriage. The horse is rented, but the carriage also bears the Granger family crest. It was a free pass, but if I go on foot, might they say something? Even though I had even simulated saying, "I'm just heading out to the city," with confidence, I passed through easily.

Perhaps the dorm students go out often.

"Well, I wonder where the shops are."

The Royal Academy is next to the Royal Palace. And just outside the school gate is the noble district. It’s all large mansions, and this isn't exactly where I want to walk around. I deduced that the further I get from the Royal Palace, the more common the area will be, so I headed south. Patience is not used to walking. I need to improve my physical stamina. Even thinking that while walking, it’s still just mansions.

"Noble mansions are just too big."

It takes minutes just to walk past one house. I couldn't reach any shops during just the fourth period. But after walking for about an hour by my estimation, the mansions started to become smaller.

"Is that a shop over there?"

I discovered my first shop in this other world! When I approached it, it was a bookstore. We have books in abundance at home, and there are plenty in the library too. But since it’s my first shop discovery, I'll go inside. Do bookstores let you read standing up? Aren't there any non-serious books, like manga? I entered with expectations, but there were only a few books, and they all seemed to be specialized texts. What a pity, let's go to the next one!

Perhaps because I was wearing the Royal Academy uniform, I wasn't scolded for walking around looking at books. At this rate, other shops seem okay too.

"I wonder if there isn't a shopping district? There are also few people walking around."

I should have exited the noble district, yet there are no people. Besides, I can't find any shops. Patience isn't used to walking, so she's getting tired. I haven't been going from one bargain sale to another, after all.

"Drat, I should have asked at that bookstore earlier."

I was nervous because it was my first shop in this other world. Besides, books are expensive, so I couldn't buy one, and it's hard to ask questions. You buy gum or something at a convenience store and then ask for directions, right?

Muttering to myself while walking for what felt like 30 minutes, I finally arrived at a place where a few shops were clustered together.

A food shop, a liquor store, a butcher, a flower shop... they don't seem suitable for research, but I'll peek into the food shop and the flower shop. As for the butcher, seeing whole carcasses hanging is impossible for both Patience and me, so I'll pass. I’ll also pass on the liquor store since I can't drink for the time being.

At the food shop, there are strawberries, but the ones in our greenhouse are redder and prettier. They are quite expensive. Should I sell them? But my little brothers like them too, so I'm conflicted.

Incidentally, eggs and sugar were also at prices the Granger family couldn't afford. I'm grateful to the Queen.

The mansion has a large garden, so maybe I should grow flowers? Thinking that, I peeked into the flower shop, and it was so expensive! Ah, that's right, it's only just become spring. If I grow flowers in the greenhouse during the winter, I might be able to make a profit!

If I can stockpile enough emergency rations by this winter, maybe I should turn half of it into roses? I thought to myself as I headed back to the academy.

Thinking idly, "I wanted to look at clothes, accessories, and small items. If I see that girl student again, maybe I'll ask her to tell me where the shops are," I was passing through the school gate when Prince Keith spotted me.

"Where have you been?"

He didn't have to lean out of the carriage window and yell, did he? It stands out.

I made up an excuse, "I was just taking a little walk," and went inside. I hope he keeps quiet about it... but Prince Keith is the type who can't read the room, after all.

"I hope he doesn't say anything in front of Queen Victoria or Princess Margaret."

I prayed to Goddess Estena, but then I remembered she didn't listen to me last time, either.

"What will be, will be!"

It's no use fretting about it. Besides, there's no guarantee that Prince Keith even remembers me.

"Besides, tomorrow

I get to see

my younger brothers!"

I thought I'd be too worried to sleep, but my tired body seemed to have slept soundly and well. I've gotten used to sleeping like this, too.

While waiting for Mary, I thought I'd ask if the horses were for rent by the morning or by the hour. Walking around to find shops is tiring and inefficient. If they were for rent in the morning, I planned to persuade Mary to take a slight detour to check out the clothing stores and such. But if I do that, the time I have with my brothers will decrease. I don't want that!

"But at least seeing the grocery store and the florist helped me understand the prices. Plus, I got some ideas on how to make money using the greenhouse..."

I had been hoping to get a horse since I could commute from home after leaving the dormitory. But it's surely impossible to quit being Princess Margaret's lady-in-waiting. If horse rentals are only for the morning, I could have Nassius train me in riding little by little.

While I was worrying about this and that, Mary came to pick me up. I felt like I had betrayed Mary somehow, and it was painful. Even if the shackles of the lady-in-waiting system are broken, I cannot betray Mary's trust.

"I can't go to town on my own. I have no choice but to involve Mary and go."

Also, I need to build up my stamina! Spring has arrived. During the hours I'm exempted from class, I must not only study, but also train Patience's body. Fortunately, the academy is extremely vast. The roses will probably be beautiful from now on. Thinking positively that taking walks would also be good, I decided to forget about the Prince Keith incident.
