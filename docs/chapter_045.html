Aoba Festival: Helping the Student Council

I listen to the Chorus Club’s performance with Princess Margaret.

"The morning session is only for primary school students, isn't it?"

She probably thought that the Chorus Club wouldn't have many audience members during the Knight Club’s matches. Oh, Louise is lined up on stage too.

"It's so-so."

Princess Margaret’s criticism is harsh, but I agree. It’s not bad, but there’s nothing particularly noteworthy about it either. It’s a well-rehearsed chorus, and they didn't hit any wrong notes, but the solo parts are lacking.

"I wonder if she was chosen because she’s a third-year? Or is it because she’s from a Count’s family? Helena’s voice doesn't have much range."

The soloist seems to be a classmate of Princess Margaret’s. I’ll keep my comments to myself.

The Music Club's second new song premiere was also wonderful, and it received a round of applause.

"The new song recital is a great success."

Princess Margaret looks happy, but I’m a little worried about the third performance.

The Upper-class Dining Hall

had a slightly different atmosphere than usual. That was because parents were there too.

"Margaret, over here."

Prince Richard is calling from in front of a folding screen. We don't usually have screens up, do we?

"The parents kept coming over to greet us one by one, so I had them put up a screen."

I see. Prince Keith was already seated as well.

"Brother, aren't you busy with the Student Council?"

Princess Margaret worries about Prince Richard, who looked at the menu and decided on "steak" immediately. It’s obvious he’s more on edge than usual.

"No, the preliminary arrangements are the troublesome part. As for today... well, a few complaints came in, but I’m managing."

Prince Keith is fidgeting, wanting to help his older brother. Prince Richard doesn't seem to notice him at all.

"The Music Club’s new song recital was very wonderful. Keith, how was the Knight Club’s match?"

Prince Keith answers with a slightly grumpy look, "Just as predicted, Department Head Eric won."

Ah, Prince Richard’s eyebrows just went up. There’s no way the Student Council President isn't busy today. On top of that, he's in a state where he can’t even eat without a screen being put up. Princess Margaret knew he was on edge when she brought it up, but he really can't read the room. I just got a signal from her eyes. Good grief.

"Can primary school students help with the Student Council, too?"

Prince Keith stops cutting his steak and looks at me. No, not me.

"I see, Patience, your recitals ended in the morning, didn't they? I'll have you help me!"

Prince Keith looks completely disappointed.

"Keith, your Knight Club match is also over, isn't it?" Princess Margaret follows up.

"That’s true. One can never have too many hands. Keith, help me out."

Oh, he’s wagging his tail as if his master just threw him a ball.

"Yes, of course!"

Um, I had free time this afternoon. I was planning on watching a play and then going to see the Alchemy Club’s presentation, but... 'Look after Prince Keith!' Princess Margaret is really good at signaling with her eyes. I guess I can't refuse. Being an attendant is tough.

"Well then, eat quickly and let's head to the Student Council room. We don't need dessert!"

I had the waiter cancel our desserts without permission and quickly left the table.

"Lady Margaret, is this alright?"

I would be leaving them behind all alone.

"Yes, Katherine and the others were there, so I'll watch the play with them. Now, hurry along."

I followed behind Prince Richard and Prince Keith. They walk fast, don't they? Is it the difference in our strides?

"Oh, Lord Ralph and Lord Hugo are also free from noon, aren't they? Prince Keith, why not invite them?"

The upper-class dining hall.

I’m dragging in the two I saw there. After all, I’m not Prince Keith’s attendant. You can take care of the trouble yourself.

"I see, then let's have the two of them help out as well."

Prince Richard gives his permission easily. Ah, he realized I was trying to make him babysit Prince Keith. He truly is a capable prince.

The student council room is so large. This spacious room was filled with the clamor of students complaining and the council members dealing with them. I want to turn around and walk right out.

"Everyone, it's too noisy! Speak calmly."

Oh, a voice of authority. It went silent, but only for a moment.

"Student Council President, our Ruffle Club has permission to use the Second Ground all day. Yet, the Riding Club's horses are eating the grass there. I told the student council member there to chase them away, but he won't listen."

A large, broad-shouldered man bellows. So noisy!

"Jeffrey, if you’re just going to complain here, you should go tell the Riding Club directly."

Prince Richard orders him away with a wave of his hand.

"Why must I be the one to ask the Riding Club? The Student Council issued the permit. You have an obligation to enforce it, don't you?"

It seems Jeffrey isn't just a muscle-head. He is logically complaining to Prince Richard.

"Understood. Keith, go to the Riding Club and have them remove the horses from the Second Ground. Ah, Ralph and Hugo, go with him."

After glancing at the three of them, he orders, "Patience, you go too." He must have thought that if Prince Keith went out of control, those two wouldn't be able to stop him. Good grief.
