The Summer Palace. It’s the sea!

I was devastated by Princess Margaret’s psychic attack, but perhaps because I’m being pulled by a 10-year-old’s body, I recovered quickly. Yes, lately I’ve been gradually getting younger. Mentally, that is. I don’t want to experience puberty twice, though… Ah, I’ve remembered all the failures of my past life.

The mood swings might also be a tendency of puberty. Ah, why am I thinking about such stupid things? It’s obviously because Prince Keith barged into our carriage.

“Big Sister Margaret, I don’t know why Big Brother Richard is angry, but I can’t stay in the same carriage as him.”

After lunch, when the carriage stopped for a break, Prince Keith had forcefully climbed in. Right now, Lady-in-waiting Charlotte is in the same carriage as the angry Prince Richard. I feel sorry for her.

“Keith, I’ve been meaning to tell you this for a while, but you make many comments that put people in a bad mood. Big Brother Richard is particularly sensitive to slips of the tongue, so you should be careful.”

Yes, that’s exactly right. But please, do something about Prince Keith, who is now in a bad mood. Hey, stop passing it off to me.

“Lady Margaret, I heard the Summer Palace is near the sea, but I’ve never seen the sea before. Is it really full of water as far as the eye can see? And is it true that sea water is salty?”

Pretending to be an innocent 10-year-old is tough for someone with a mental age of 25.

“You, have you never seen the sea? By any chance, can you not swim?”

Prince Keith’s mood improved as he teased me, but if he doesn’t fix that kind of behavior, he’ll provoke Prince Richard’s wrath again.

“Oh my, Patience can’t swim? That won’t do. Keith, you must teach her.”

“Yes, Big Sister. Even Jane can swim a little. Besides, if you fall into the water and can’t swim, you’ll die.”

I want to avoid learning how to swim from an eager Prince Keith, but I used to swim in my past life, so I should be able to manage.

“Oh, I can smell the sea now. Look, Patience, you can see the sea!”

Mary, please don’t scold me for leaning out of the carriage. It’s Prince Keith’s fault, after all.

“Ah, is that the sea? Isn’t it small?”

“You’re such a fool, it only looks small because it’s still far away. You’ll be surprised by its size when we get much closer.”

Prince Keith, you’re in a better mood now that you’re bragging to me about the sea. I’m glad.

The sea was big. I know that. But it’s my first time seeing the sea in this alternate world. Besides, it’s beautiful.

“You don’t have to stare at it so hard; the sea isn’t going to run away. Look, we’re arriving.”

I had been looking only at the sea, but I was surprised when I turned my eyes to the Summer Palace. It’s huge!

The procession of carriages stopped, and the Queen entered the palace. Of course, we followed her. Mary hurried to her room with the other ladies-in-waiting and maids, along with the luggage. Princess Jane and Prince Marcus are with their tutors and nannies here, too.

I wait in the salon until the room is ready. Naturally, the Queen’s room is prepared immediately, and she heads there with her lady-in-waiting. Left behind are the ill-humored Prince Richard, the spirited Prince Keith, and Princess Margaret. Please stop signaling me with your eyes to "do something" about it.

「The sea is really vast, isn't it? But is it truly salty? Oh, is that why salt is made from the sea?」

Three pairs of stunned eyes are turned toward me. Oh, was that too staged?

「Patience, everyone knows salt comes from rock salt. You really are ignorant of the world.」

I am scolded by Princess Margaret.

「Eh? Can't you make salt by boiling seawater? I know about rock salt, but it seems sea salt is mellower and more delicious.」

Prince Richard takes the bait.

「Where did you hear that salt can be obtained from seawater?」

「Um, that is... I feel like I read it somewhere, but is that wrong?」

I am mocked by Prince Keith.

「Salt is harvested at the rock salt mines on Mount Halla. You may be good at studying, but you are quite ignorant.」

Ah, is that so. I didn't know about Mount Halla.

「But the reason rock salt is found on Mount Halla is that it was once the sea, right? The sea rose to become a mountain, and the seawater there solidified. That is why salt can be made from seawater.」

Prince Richard begins to think seriously.

「I see, seawater is salty. If you heat it, the water evaporates and the salt remains!」

Eh, in this other world, was it only rock salt? Oh no, I've made a mistake.

「Patience, let's experiment.」

Well, I suppose it’s fine that Prince Richard’s mood has improved, but this doesn't seem like a simple matter. Salt monopolies and national interests, right? It seems like there would be huge profits involved, but if someone like the Granger family, who have been dismissed from service, gets involved, they'll be blown away. So, I would like just a tiny bit of an "idea fee," if possible.

Ah, I want money. I can't do side work at the summer palace. Besides, the backyard garden is left entirely to George.

『I see, if I make some salt and take it home, I can save a little money. Salt is necessary for preserved food, after all.』

It’s very petty compared to Prince Richard, who is trying to make salt for the sake of the country, but the Granger family is poor.

Princess Margaret's room is made ready, the princes depart, and finally, Mary comes to call for me.

「My lady, sorry to have kept you waiting.」

「You didn't have to rush so much. I was just watching the sea from the window.」

Yes, I was looking at the seawater that will become money. At the very least, I want to make enough salt for the Granger family to use. The problem is how to take it home. How much can fit in a wardrobe trunk?

It seems my worries are trivial. Despite having come to another world, I have no connection to adventure or making a fortune. Is it perhaps because I can only use life magic?
