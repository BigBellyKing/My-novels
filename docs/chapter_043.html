The Day Before the Aoba Festival

There were no classes at the academy on the day before the Aoba Festival either. From the morning, we discussed the arrangements for the three new song performances at the Music Club. I'm a second-year student, so I just listen silently. It seems difficult for students whose parents are coming to visit to coordinate with those times.

"There is a Knight Club match in the morning. People will be gathering there."

It would be impossible to decide if we had to consider the performances of other clubs too. The times when the Music Club can use the auditorium have been notified by the Student Council. The time allotted is the same as the Theater Club and the Chorus Club, but the difference is that the Theater Club takes theirs all in one block.

"The Theater Club is taking three hours after the lunch break. It's unfair."

The Music Club gets three one-hour slots, and the Chorus Club is the same. I heard that a long time ago they used to take three hours at a time, but since it's hard to play or sing for three hours continuously, it became the current method. Because there is a Knight Club match in the morning, the Music Club, which has two morning slots, ended up at a disadvantage this year. The fact that the Chorus Club only has one slot in the morning is the cause of the dissatisfaction.

"The schedule rotates by year. Next year, the Chorus Club will have two slots in the morning, so we just have to be patient this year."

Club President Melissa summarizes. Complaining won't get us anywhere.

"Write your names in your preferred times, and let's adjust the ones with too many people."

Everyone writes them down, but as expected, the afternoon is popular.

"It's troublesome that some say they want the afternoon because their parents want to watch the Knight Club match. I want to keep it to five people each."

I wrote my name in the first morning slot, the same as Princess Margaret. The King and Queen cannot come to observe, and Princess Margaret has no interest in the Knight Club. Although Catherine and the others are usually her entourage, they seem to have students they are interested in in the Knight Club, so they are using their parents as an excuse to hope for the afternoon.

"It's fine if it's not exactly five people each. We can just play several songs during the slots with fewer people."

Albert, wondering what could be so interesting about the Knight Club, had also put his name in the second morning slot, but he also writes his name in the first. President Melissa also wrote her name in the first and second slots.

"I'll cooperate too."

Three male students also changed to the morning, and it was finally decided.

"It will be eight people in the afternoon, but that's fine, right? If you have any complaints, say them now. I won't accept any later."

President Melissa is so cool! She has a real sense of chivalry.

"Now, decide which songs you'll play for each number. Make sure they don't overlap. Basically, it will be your own new song and someone else's song."

She claps her hands and dismisses us. The first slot includes Princess Margaret, President Melissa, Vice President Albert, and me—the four of us. The second slot includes President Melissa, Vice President Albert, and three male students—five in total.

The third slot in the afternoon consists of eight girls only.

Somehow, the third slot in the afternoon looks like it will lead to trouble, but I should focus on deciding the first slot.

"I'll play my new song and Patience's 'Minuet'."

Albert liked it so much he almost proposed to her.

"Oh my, I wanted to play the 'Minuet' too."

Princess Margaret also liked the 'Minuet' and had been practicing it.

"Then, Albert, play it during the second slot. I'll play my new song and Catherine's new song."

Albert puts in his check.

"Is that 'Für Elise' you got from Patience? I suppose I have no choice. Then, I shall play 'Eine kleine Nachtmusik'."

Everyone was picking new songs one after another. This is troublesome. I want to play Princess Margaret's song, but since she is playing it herself, that won't do.

"What are you going to play, Patience? 'Minuet' is out, as it's already being performed twice this morning. Oh my, you have many songs in the second segment, too. Choose one that doesn't overlap."

For the second segment, Albert chose 'Minuet', Department Head Melissa chose 'Turkish March', Benjamin chose 'Träumerei', and Helmut chose 'Twinkle Twinkle Little Star Variations'.

"I will play 'Twinkle Twinkle Little Star Variations' and 'Minute Waltz'."

The morning schedule was somehow decided, but the third segment in the afternoon was still causing conflict. Even though I wished to play the masterpieces from my previous life, Catherine and the other two had already claimed them for their own slots. So, there were only four songs left. Eight people were fighting over them.

"That is why I told you to compose more, but you wouldn't listen to me."

For some reason, I was scolded by Princess Margaret. I can't accept this. I provided seven songs, including the ones I gave away. Hmph.

"If there had been just one more song, they wouldn't be fighting so much."

"As if I care!" I wanted to yell, but I held it in tightly.

"Department Head Melissa will probably manage to sort it out. More importantly, since there are fewer people in the first segment, I have to play two more songs. I will play 'Turkish March'. As for the rest, Melissa or Princess Margaret can play something."

Princess Margaret was taken aback by selfish Albert.

"I am fine. Couldn't Albert just play four songs?"

"Hmm, what shall I play?" he pondered seriously, but was scolded by Department Head Melissa.

"Melissa and Albert are appearing in the first and second segments. We will have Princess Margaret and Patience play three songs each here."

Since Albert was removed from the list, Princess Margaret declared, "I will play 'Turkish March'."

I thought for a moment and chose 'Träumerei', which I had given to Lilina. I like this song, after all.

In the end, the third segment was debated so fiercely that Department Head Melissa's thunderous rage struck down.

"First, decide on your own new songs. For the rest, we will draw lots for the shorter pieces. I will not hear any complaints."

While other time slots have about 10 songs, will the third segment really have 16 performances? There are many short pieces, but I have a feeling that we might run over time and end up in a quarrel with the Chorus Club. I'll stay away from the auditorium in the afternoon.
