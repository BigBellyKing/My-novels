Improving health with a jump rope!

Tea time is over, but Henry is excited about the sweets, which he’s barely had before. When Patience and Nashius were young, they were still living a normal aristocratic life, but after they were dismissed from service right after Henry was born, he has known nothing but a life of poverty. It’s possible this was even his first time eating sugar.

“Henry, you’re too loud.”

Nashius, who is reading, warns Henry, who is running around the nursery, but he doesn't stop his frantic dashing.

“He must be experiencing a sugar high.”

It’s not a phrase you hear much in Japan, but in American family dramas, there are scenes where children eat sweets at night and get too rowdy to sleep. It’s because their blood sugar levels have spiked and they’re hyped up. At this rate, he might not even be able to sleep tonight.

That’s right, there’s something good for exercising in the nursery even on days with bad weather. And besides, I can make that with things already in the manor.

I had George make six handles and made three jump ropes. I take Henry, who is running around, and Nashius, who only ever reads books, out into the garden.

“Sister, what is this?”

“Is it a toy?”

It’s the first jump rope in this other world. Patience has never done it, of course. Even I haven't done it since middle school.

“You jump like this.”

But, things I once learned, I haven't forgotten.

“First, the basic jump. Then this is the running jump. The crossover. The double under.”

Huff, puff—Patience needs exercise too. Maybe she died of pneumonia because she lacked this much stamina. Though the cold was a factor, too.

“Henry, you’re good at it. Nashius, don’t give up, if you practice you’ll be able to do it.”

Perhaps Henry feels like he’s using body reinforcement magic. I wonder if Nashius has the same wind affinity as his father? I want to save up money and have them get their ability assessments as soon as possible. I would love to watch the two of them continue practicing jump rope, undefeated by the cold wind, but I have things to do. I need to make other sweets.

I write down the recipes for cookies and pound cake and give them to Eva.

“If there’s anything you don’t understand, ask me.”

Mary won’t let me get near the fire, so I just hand over the recipes and watch.

For dessert at dinner, we had thinly sliced pound cake. Eva is good at slicing things thinly. Well, it would be troublesome if Henry got a sugar high before bed, so maybe this amount is better?

“It’s Sunday. I have to go back to the dormitory, don’t I?”

It’s gloomy from the morning. But, to change the mood, I plant the strawberry seeds Mary bought for me with my younger brothers.

“Sister, aren’t strawberries harvested in the spring?”

Yeah, Nashius is smart.

“Yes, that’s right. But since it’s a greenhouse, I’m sure they’ll grow.”

“Are strawberries delicious?”

“Henry, you’ve had them before, haven’t you?”

I was surprised.

“Perhaps he’s forgotten because he was so young. He hasn't eaten any in these past few years.”

I see, fruit has been on the back burner, too.

“When the strawberries are ready, let’s pick them together.”

I was having a good time with my younger brothers when Mary came to call me.

“My lady, a lady-in-waiting has arrived from the royal palace. Oh, you’re covered in dirt!”

“It’s fine,” I say to a flustered Mary, and use life magic to show her, “Cleanse!”

"That is good. I have them waiting in the drawing room. Please hurry."

I hurried, thinking she had left them waiting in a drawing room without a fire, but Wyatt had George lighting a fire. Though it is still cold.

"Sorry to have kept you waiting. I am Patience Granger."

Even when in a hurry, a young lady must not let it show. I greeted her as if I were accustomed to welcoming court ladies from the royal palace.

"I am Charlotte Evans. I am a court lady serving at the royal palace. Today, I have come to deliver a gift from Her Majesty the Queen."

A gift? I've already received a basket full of eggs and sugar, haven't I?

"Please read this."

The letter handed to me read, "Please practice your Hanon and let me hear a new piece again." Ugh, I feel depressed thinking that I have to go to the royal palace again.

"Where shall I put the Hanon?"

I was surprised by the court lady's question.

"Eh? A place to put the Hanon?"

"Yes, was it not written in Queen Victoria's letter? She was pleased with your Hanon performance, Lady Patience, and requested that you practice it."

While I was bewildered, my butler Wyatt gave instructions to the royal servants to set up the Hanon by the window in the drawing room. Through Patience's memories, I realized that was where the Hanon had been placed before.

"I will write a thank-you letter to the Queen. Please wait a moment."

In my own room, relying on Patience's etiquette, I wrote a thank-you letter and had the court lady take it back with her.

"I've managed to get my hands on a Hanon, but... I feel like I've fallen deep into a trap."

I became anxious that I might not be able to quit as Princess Margaret's lady-in-waiting. Moreover, it is terrifying that the Queen knew I didn't have a Hanon at home.

"I had thought that I wanted to avoid getting involved with the royalty as much as possible..."

Thinking about the future won't change how things turn out. After all, I never even imagined I would end up in another world.

"Now, Nasius, Henry, let's practice the Hanon."

I changed my mood by teaching the two of them scales. If I didn't do something like that, I wouldn't be able to endure the week at the dormitory.
