Aoba Festival Alchemy Club

Just as I was about to leave the student council room saying, "Excuse me," Prince Keith called out to me.

"Patience, the Aoba Festival must be lonely on your own. Let's go around together."

He likely thought it was pitiful for me to be alone since I am usually with Princess Margaret, but his concern is unsolicited.

"No, I am fine."

"There is no need to be reserved. Now, where shall we go first?"

It isn't reservation; I am refusing him from the heart, but Prince Keith has no ears to listen.

"Come to think of it, Sister Margaret mentioned that the Music Club's new song recital is wonderful. Let's head to the auditorium."

Perhaps Prince Keith was trying to be considerate in his own way, but I have a hunch that the third [song/performance] might cause trouble with the Chorus Club, so I don't want to go near it.

"Prince Keith, please head to the Music Club's recital. I have heard it many times during practice, so I will visit other places."

Inside my head, Patience is shouting, "How rude!", but I want to keep my distance from trouble.

I don't want to pull chestnuts out of the fire. But roasted chestnuts do sound delicious. I'm indulging in such escapism because Prince Keith is exercising his chivalry and following me, saying, "Then, let's go together."

"Which exhibition are we going to see?"

I hate being with Prince Keith, who has a crowd of attendants trailing behind him, because it draws attention, but now that it’s come to this, I just want to at least accomplish my goal.

"The Alchemy Club's exhibition."

Prince Keith makes a strange face. He probably imagined something like the Art Club or the Handicraft Club.

"You really are strange. Besides, next year you'll be skipping ahead to the middle division and taking the Home Economics course and the Civil Official course, right? Surely you aren't also taking the Mage course?"

Ralph and Hugo seem unaware of the grade skipping and the dual-course selection, and are surprised. Seriously, Prince Keith, don't say unnecessary things.

"Of course not, I am just interested in alchemy."

Prince Keith is saying something rude like, "You really are a strange woman."

"Patience, taking two courses—is that even possible?"

Hugo looks worried.

"What are you talking about? My older brother is taking both the Knight course and the Civil Official course. I intend to follow his example."

Ralph and Hugo are questioning Prince Keith, asking, "Are you serious?" Their two classmates might end up having to take two courses as well. They can't afford to be concerned with someone like me.

"Ah, this is the Alchemy Club."

There are no visitors, so it's a little hard to enter.

"Let's look quickly and then head to the dance venue."

The dance venue? I was planning to pass on that...

"Ah, are you here for a tour?"

We were spotted by the Alchemy Club members. No, I wanted to take a tour, so it should be fine, but I somehow feel like running away. They are wearing white lab coats over their Royal Academy uniforms, but they are incredibly filthy. They are at a level where I'd want to use life magic to clean them.

Oh, Ralph and Hugo are standing in front of Prince Keith in a guard formation.

"Yes, we are here for a tour."

The three boys, who are visibly shrinking back, sent me looks that said, "Are you seriously going to tour this place?" but I ignored them. After all, even if the member explaining things looks like a mad scientist, I am interested in the exhibits.

"Could this, by any chance, be an electric fan?"

Three blades were spinning inside a box, and a little bit of breeze was coming out.

"That's right. You figured it out quickly, didn't you? By any chance, do you want to join the Alchemy Club?"

Both of my hands were suddenly grabbed. Hugo helped me out by saying, "Let her go!"

"I'm already in the Music Club, and I haven't learned alchemy yet, so it's impossible."

"If you're interested in alchemy, I'll teach you. Practice is more important than the lessons, you know."

The club members who were sitting in the corner of the room also came over to recruit me.

"That's right! There is no club that contributes to society as much as alchemy. Besides, if you can develop a new magic tool, you can even get a patent."

A patent! I could earn money.

"Can a complete amateur really do alchemy?"

I was quite swayed by this.

"Hey, are you serious? You should consult your sister before deciding. This place is a gathering of eccentrics."

Having been warned by Prince Keith, I calmed down a bit. Being weak to money is a flaw of mine, isn't it? Patents aren't that easy to get, anyway.

"If you feel like joining, I'm at the Alchemy Club every day from Monday to Friday after school, so come by. I am Caesar Burns. I'm the club president."

The exhibits were just different versions of electric fans, so we finished the tour and went out into the hallway.

"If you join a club like that, you won't be able to find anyone to marry you."

Prince Keith looked at me in disbelief.

"I have no intention of getting married, so it's fine."

All three of them looked at me in disbelief. Ah, how cute. At ten years old, they are just my type as a shotacon. No, I won't lay a hand on them!

"You won't get married? Are you, by any chance, going to become a nun? Can you even become a nun with just life magic?"

It seems Prince Keith was worried about me.

"I have no intention of becoming a nun. I intend to work."

Prince Keith is satisfied, saying, "Well, you are Princess Margaret's attendant, after all," but I would like to refrain from that.

"Are you going to serve Princess Margaret for your whole life? But what will you do when she gets married?"

Ralph was also worried. Even if the King abdicates, a prince just becomes the King's brother, but a princess's circumstances change upon marriage.

"There is also the possibility that she will marry into a foreign country, you know."

Hugo also looks worried. They don't have to worry so much about me serving for life, because I have no intention of doing so.

"No, I don't mean as an attendant; I intend to work."

The three of them looked at me in disbelief again.

"As I thought, you are strange. But if you join something like the Alchemy Club, you'll become even stranger, so don't do it. Come on, let's go to the dance hall."

I called out to the three of them as they walked away quickly.

"I'll refrain from dancing."

I thought I would do some side work making handkerchiefs in the dormitory until dinner, but I was dragged along.

"It's fine even if you don't have a partner."

Huh? Does everyone have a partner? I was too busy with my studies and attending to Princess Margaret to know that. I really don't want to go.
