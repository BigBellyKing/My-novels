Barnes Trading Company

How delightful! This is my first outing since sneaking out of the academy to peek into the bookstore, the greengrocer, and the florist. Visits to places like the Earl of Northcourt's house don't count—that’s a different kind of meaning! Mary can’t complain either, since Department Head Caesar and Benjamin are with us.

The Barnes Duke’s mansion was located in the high-ranking nobility area. Yes, the Granger family also has a mansion in this area. I suppose they must have received a proper salary in the past.

After riding in the carriage for a while, the mansions became a bit smaller. That said, they are still the size of mansions from my past life. A little further on, the front gardens began to disappear. The houses are packed tightly together. It looks like the streets of Paris. It seems like there might be courtyards, but along the road, there’s a row of apartment buildings with the same style. I wonder if this area is where the lower nobility or wealthy commoners live?

“That over there is the Romano main store of the Barnes Trading Company.”

Well, it’s impressive. It looks like an old, established European department store. It blends into the townscape, but the decorations on the pillars and the window display on the first floor catch the eye. It’s trying its best for an "other world," but looking at it as someone raised in a city in my past life, it’s a bit plain or perhaps unsophisticated. It’s just lining up best-selling products. Needs improvement!

While thinking that, I still check it out thoroughly. I want to know what’s selling. Hmm, hmm, since it’s winter, the clothing on display is coats. And for magic tools, there are magic lamps; they also sell armor and swords. And tableware and pots too... well, they sell everything except groceries. Indeed, as a window display, it’s not very polished, but maybe it’s a good thing that you can tell what they’re selling.

“Come on, we’re going inside!”

Department Head Caesar urges me on while I’m staring at the window in front of the store. Yes, he opened the door and held it for me here, too. His manners are well-ingrained.

“The first floor is for tableware, pots, and various tools. The second floor is mainly for clothing, armor, and textile products. The third floor has many expensive items like magic tools and swords.”

It seems the higher the floor, the more expensive the products. The first floor is lined with cooking utensils and tableware, but there’s also a corner with inexpensive cloaks and the like.

I wanted to take my time looking around, but Caesar and Benjamin quickly went up to the second floor. Do men hate shopping? Even if they don’t hate it, they just seem to look at what they want to buy. Yes, because they even breezed past the second floor and headed straight to the magic tool section on the third floor.

“Oh, they’ve got quite a variety on display.”

I’m interested in magic tools, too. But I also wanted to look at clothes and such. Hmph! Having said that, I end up looking at the magic tools myself.

“As expected, there are a lot of magic lamps.”

Caesar and Benjamin skip over the area where the magic lamps are placed.

That makes sense; they are lined with the mass-produced models we assembled in Alchemy 1. The price is reasonably high, but it’s not an amount I can’t afford. Yes, I could probably even buy one with money earned from embroidery side jobs.

Mary is also looking from behind me. Maybe she wants one? Magic lamps are definitely easier for reading and needlework than candles. Magic lamps can be made with alchemy, but the magic stone is the problem.

“Is the price of magic stones in the Lawrence Kingdom higher than in other countries?”

At my question, Caesar, who was looking at other magic tools, turns around and tilts his head.

“Well, I’ve never thought about the price of magic stones, so I don’t know. But, Patience, do you know something?”

Rich boy! He’s never had to struggle with magic stones.

“It’s one of my homework assignments for diplomacy studies. The task is to consider what we can do to get our country’s culture recognized abroad. Mr. Fochtner said that even though the Lawrence Kingdom is the one making the magic tools, the country that uses them the most is the Sonia Kingdom. I thought perhaps magic stones might be cheaper in the Sonia Kingdom.”

Benjamin didn’t know if the price of magic stones was high or low compared to foreign countries either.

“It seems like Manager Powell would be well-informed about these kinds of things.”

Even though I still wanted to look around, I was dragged to the manager’s office.

“Oh my, Lord Caesar, Lord Benjamin. Was there some magic tool you wanted?”

It seems Benjamin comes to the Barnes Trading Company quite often.

“Powell, can you spare us a moment? This is Patience Granger. From now on, the hot water bottles and threaders that Patience created will be manufactured and sold by the Barnes Trading Company.”

Powell smiles brightly and greets me.

“My name is Max Powell. It is a pleasure to meet you, Lady Patience.”

I return the greeting and sit on the sofa I was offered. Mary is also offered a seat, so she sits on the wooden chair behind the sofa where we are seated.

“We came because I have a question to ask you, Powell. Is the price of magic stones in our country higher than in foreign countries?”

Powell looked a bit surprised.

“I’m surprised that Lord Caesar is interested in business matters; I hope it’s not because something bad has happened. Joking aside, it is true that our country’s magic stones are priced 20 percent higher than in the Sonia Kingdom. Where did you hear about this?”

20 percent higher! That’s 120 percent! Is such a ridiculous price even allowed?

“They’re that expensive?!”

Caesar and Benjamin are also surprised.

“Yes, I don’t think there’s that much difference in the number of monsters or the number of them being subjugated... I see, it must be imports from the Southern Continent. But I heard the Cordoba Kingdom is very active in imports?”

Caesar is very knowledgeable. Benjamin didn’t seem to know.

“Are there a lot of monsters on the Southern Continent?” he asks.

“Well, I don’t know about that, but the Southern Continent’s exports are magic stones and spices. Benjamin, you really should take the civil official course. You have far too little common sense.”

Benjamin grumbles, “That’s so harsh,” but it seems Department Head Caesar studies even without taking the civil official course.

“Since magic tools are not very widespread in the Cordoba Kingdom, they must be exporting them to the Sonia Kingdom.”

I see, so the Sonia Kingdom isn't just about romance. They are also proactive in enjoying a cultural life. I’m not interested in romance yet, but I would like to visit the Sonia Kingdom. I don't want to go back to Omar ever again, so the Cordoba Kingdom, where magic tools aren't widespread, is a pass for me. I also want to pass on the Cordoba language, where saliva goes flying when you talk.

“Oh, then why doesn’t the Lawrence Kingdom import them too?”

I think it’s strange, so I ask.

“Ah, that’s because a princess of the Sonia Kingdom married into the Cordoba Kingdom. I heard rumors that King Carlos is head-over-heels for the beautiful Queen Leonora. They must have lowered the tariffs.”

For a reason like that? It’s shocking!

“Well, our country does import magic stones, but there are tariffs, and it seems they are still expensive for the common people.”

That’s the truth! Couldn’t magic stones be cheaper? But then again, there were no magic stones in my past life.

“Lord Caesar, Lord Benjamin, we must create convenient tools that don’t use magic stones!”

“That wouldn’t be a magic tool, would it!”

“Magic circles and magic stones are a set, you know.”

I was looked at with amazement by the two. But Powell was pleased.

“That is correct. By all means, please do so.”

I remind the two who were dumbfounded, “Bicycles don’t use magic stones, you know.”

“Ah, that’s a good point.”

“That’s right, I wonder if the Barnes Trading Company has materials for tires?”

They’re so self-centered; since they like bicycles, that’s fine. But I also want the tires to be improved, so the three of us look around everywhere.

“Lord Powell, do you know of any plants on the Southern Continent that produce sticky sap? Or perhaps you know of anything with elasticity?”

Powell is also tilting his head.

“I have never been to the Southern Continent, so I’m sorry.”

I was hoping there would be a rubber tree, but even if there was, I have no way of getting one.

“Since they sold slime powder, let’s try it with that. We also have leather for the seat.”

“Lord Caesar, we also need some kind of cushion.”

Department Head Caesar and Benjamin are absorbed in the bicycle.

“Oh, it would be easier to hold if we wrapped leather around the handlebars too.”

Well, I’m quite looking forward to it, too.

“My Lady,” Mary pulls my sleeve silently.

“Oh my, it’s already this time. I have to go to the dormitory.”

In the end, I couldn't see the tableware, pots, or clothes. What a pity.

I was sent back by Department Head Caesar and Benjamin. My father was a little surprised that Benjamin came to pay his respects, but I have no time for that. Department Head Caesar handed the contract to my father, but the explanation will be next week.

I want to get back before Princess Margaret arrives at the dormitory. It’s really bothersome to have them come to pick me up for greetings, and then greet them again when they drop me off.

Though I don’t forget to kiss my younger brothers even when I’m in a hurry.

Somehow, I barely made it to the dormitory before Princess Margaret. Phew.
