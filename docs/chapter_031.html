Music Club

There's no time to be depressed about dancing and such. I have to hurry to Class 3-A.

"Patience, let me introduce you to the members of the Music Club."

A few schoolmates were sitting around Princess Margaret in the classroom. If even one of these people had joined the dormitory, I would have resented the fact that I wouldn't have had to become a lady-in-waiting.

"This is Catherine Woodstock, the second daughter of the Marquis of Woodstock. That over there is Liliana Claridge. She's the eldest daughter of Count Claridge, right? And Harriet Linderman. Was it the fourth daughter of Count Linderman?"

"Princess Margaret, I am the third daughter."

Harriet corrects her with a laugh. I am busy bowing and saying "Pleasure to meet you" every time I am introduced. Wait, only three?

There are still several people left in the classroom, though? Oh, I see, they aren't in the Music Club.

"Now, let's go to the Music Club."

Catherine, who was introduced first, started walking briskly. Her hair is a deep, curly blonde. This hairstyle would be impossible in dormitory life. Harriet is walking arm-in-arm with Princess Margaret. Light blonde hair with light blue eyes. Fluffy curls. Liliana is following behind them. She's like a fairy. Silver hair with violet eyes. Her silver hair is straight and glistening. Yep, I can tell that all of you have your hair very well taken care of by your maids.

Envious eyes are cast upon the schoolmates, who were chosen for both their status and their looks. Ah, it seems like they don't even notice a little lady-in-waiting like me. That's great, really!

I'd never been to the clubhouse before because I had no connection to it, but this is different from the club activities I was imagining. This is a salon. And a high-class one at that. Even though they are wearing uniforms, it's like a completely different world. The chairs and sofas even have cabriole legs. A dozen or so people were sitting there elegantly, chatting and laughing.

"Princess Margaret, is this the new club member you recommended?"

A middle school student calls out. It's not just her schoolmates, even the club members call her "Princess Margaret." Since I am a lady-in-waiting, I'll stay low-key.

"Yes, Melissa. You arrived at just the right time. Everyone, this is my lady-in-waiting, Patience Granger. I am recommending her for the Music Club."

Melissa smiled brightly and reached out to shake my hand. It's my first time shaking hands.

"Welcome to the Music Club. Here, we don't worry about status; we freely compete in music. I am the club president, Melissa Vermond. This is the vice president, Albert Laforet."

Melissa is an exotic beauty with dark brown hair. Albert is a young man who looks exactly like an artist. His brown hair is as long as a girl's and well-maintained. It doesn't look like he has any split ends.

"Hey, let's get right to it—I want you to play something for us."

Whoa, Catherine's preemptive strike has arrived. As Princess Margaret's closest schoolmate, does she find a lowly lady-in-waiting like me unacceptable?

"That's right, I'd like to hear it too."

Harriet seems like a spoiled girl, but she looks quite malicious. Liliana nods silently.

"Well then, Patience, please play the new song I gave you yesterday."

Eh, but I've only played it once... I'll play the Hanon, relying on my patience.

“Well, as expected of an attendant chosen by Queen Victoria.”

It seems I passed by Catherine’s standards.

“But, I would have liked a bit more emotion. Playing exactly as the sheet music dictates is a bit dull.”

Albert chimed in with his assessment.

“Well, I only played it once yesterday, so it can’t be helped. You shouldn't be mean just because it’s your composition, Albert.”

Upon Princess Margaret’s words, everyone cast a scrutinizing “Hmm” look toward me.

“But, one must be able to compose, mustn’t one?”

Harriet, you may look like a cute, fluffy girl, but you’re quite devious at heart. The fact that you’d show that side of yourself proves you’re still young. Well, you are two years older than Patience, though.

“Indeed, I understand expecting new songs from a new member, but let’s wait until she’s a bit more accustomed to things.”

As expected of Club President Melissa! She’s great at bringing everyone together. After that, I listened to the other members playing the Hanon, lute, and flute one after another. Everyone is so talented! Clap, clap.

I was worried that ambitious Louise might switch from the Chorus Club because Princess Margaret is in the Music Club, but that seems quite difficult. I suspect the only reason I was able to join is because of Princess Margaret’s recommendation.

I was sitting in a chair in the corner of the clubhouse, feeling relieved that just listening to music would be easy. Well, I knew it wouldn't end that simply.

“Patience, play one more song—anything will do.”

I received an unreasonable demand from Princess Margaret. All the new songs I received last night had already been played. Plus, the songs I knew had also been played extremely well.

Whatever! A woman needs guts. I decided to play a song from my previous life. A sonata by the musical genius, Mozart. I chose it because it’s short, bright, and cheerful. Plus, I have it memorized. The keyboard on this Hanon is slightly different from a piano, so I made a tiny mistake.

“I apologize. I made a touch error.”

It’s silent. I made a mistake and offended them. I apologize in a hurry.

“No, a little touch error like that is no problem at all. You—Patience, was it? That’s an amazing talent.”

Eek! Albert is kneeling beside me while I’m sitting in front of the Hanon, and he’s kissing my hand. I’m turning bright red from embarrassment. I haven’t even experienced this in my previous life. I wish he’d let go of my hand. Wait, he’s rubbing his cheek against it. Seriously, stop!

“Albert, I will not permit you to act so presumptuously toward my attendant.”

Princess Margaret saved my hand from Albert. I felt relieved, but I can also feel intense gazes from Catherine, Harriet, and Lilina. It seems I’ve been upgraded from a “tiny attendant” to a “rival.”

“I look forward to your future new songs as well. However, Patience, your lack of practice was noticeable. You must practice enough on the Hanon in your room.”

Oh no! It looks like my required hours are going to increase. Sigh.
