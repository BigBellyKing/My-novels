Being a personal attendant is... exhausting, isn't it?

I thought the dinner bell saved me, but sigh, I was wrong.

"Oh, it's dinner time, is it? Then, let's continue our conversation after dinner."

It seems I have to be a conversational partner even after dinner. And I'm scared of how many demands there will be. It's tough that we're even in the same club after school. Club activities aren't every day, right?

I followed Princess Margaret down from the third floor, walking slightly behind her. I imitated how Mary always accompanies her like this.

There is a proverb that says "walk three steps behind and never step on your master's shadow," but if I stay that far back, I can't be a conversational partner.

The moment I got down to the dining hall, Prince Richard invited me, "Margaret, let's eat together." That's a high hurdle for the first day.

"Brother, thank you. That's perfect. This is Patience Granger, the personal attendant Mother chose for me."

Huh? Somehow, that sounds different from what it meant a moment ago.

"I see, if Mother chose her, I'm relieved. Patience, make sure you serve Margaret well."

"I am Patience Granger. Pleased to meet you," I greeted him. Prince Richard obediently accepts the personal attendant Queen Victoria chose. These kinds of rules are hard for me to understand.

I stood in line behind Prince Richard and Princess Margaret, carrying my tray. Ah, I stand out somehow. Perhaps royalty doesn't mind because they are always the center of attention, but it's tiring.

Just when I thought I finally sat down, Prince Keith joined us, saying, "Brother Richard, Sister Margaret, I'll join you too."

"Why are you here?"

Princess Margaret explained, "Patience is my personal attendant chosen by Mother."

Prince Keith acted all high and mighty, saying, "Make sure you take good care of her," but he didn't object. Well, I suppose he has no interest in his sister's personal attendant.

I didn't feel like I really ate dinner while being with royalty.

After dinner, I asked about the days for club activities in Princess Margaret's room.

"They are on Tuesdays and Thursdays. On those days, come to my classroom. Oh, that's right. Play a little Hanon for me."

It seems she wants to check how well I can play Hanon. If I play poorly, will the confinement time be shortened? But Patience is serious, so she wouldn't slack off.

"My, you're truly skilled. With this, you won't bring shame to the music club."

Since it was night, it was just one piece, but on Mondays, Wednesdays, and Fridays when there is no club, I might be background music duty. Sigh.

I was finally given permission to "withdraw." So I can't leave unless I'm given permission. I feel like crying.

Patience is making a fuss about it being a breach of manners, but I flop down onto the sofa.

"I'm exhausted."

I have no energy left to read the textbooks for the second year I skipped into. I'll take a bath and go to sleep.

Clang, clang

I usually wake up before the bell rings, but it seems I was fast asleep due to the mental exhaustion of last night. I'm dumbfounded by the habit-bound nature of Patience, who sleeps elegantly even when fast asleep.

"Could it be that I have to go and pick up Princess Margaret?"

Patience is making a fuss saying, "Of course," so I quickly wash my face and get changed. Since I’ve been trained, I’m fast at this.

I hurry to the third floor and knock on the luxurious door of Special Room 1. I waited for Princess Margaret to respond, but there is no reply.

"It’s not like she’s still asleep, right?"

As her attendant, I received the key to Special Room 1 from Princess Margaret last night. Don't tell me, was it for this? Eating breakfast together is a duty required of me as her attendant. Trembling, I open the door with the key. Princess Margaret isn't there. Is she sleeping? No, she must be getting changed.

I try greeting her with "Good morning," but there is no reaction.

My wish for her to be awake was betrayed. I knock on the bedroom door.

"Princess Margaret, good morning. It’s Patience."

I wake her up in a rather loud voice.

"Morning..."

It seems she has woken up. Good grief. Didn't she hear the bell? I wish she would get changed before the breakfast bell rings... Wait, it’s quiet, isn't it? Don't tell me she went back to sleep!

Even after knocking, it remains quiet. Is waking someone up also part of an attendant's job? But if I don't get her up and dressed, I won't be able to eat breakfast with her.

"Princess Margaret, excuse me."

Wow, she’s really sleeping. Is it because royalty is used to having maids and court ladies always by their side that she can sleep while someone enters? Now is not the time to be thinking such laid-back thoughts. The breakfast bell has rung. If things keep going like this, I'll miss out on eating too.

"Princess Margaret, please wake up!"

I wake Princess Margaret up with a loud voice.

"Good morning. The breakfast bell has already rung. Please get ready quickly."

I urge Princess Margaret, who sluggishly gets out of bed, to hurry.

"Patience, please do my hair."

After washing her face and finally putting on her uniform, Princess Margaret sits in front of the luxurious vanity, looking dazed. It seems she has low blood pressure.

"Would the hairstyle from last night be alright?"

I quickly brush her hair with a brush that has a miniature painting on the back, pull it into a half-up style, and secure it with a hair clip that was left on the vanity.

"Now, let's go to the dining hall."

Quite some time has passed since the bell rang. I usually go to the dining hall right away, so I'm worried about whether we can still eat.

"You don't need to be in such a hurry," Princess Margaret says nonchalantly.

We managed to make it just in time, but most of the students had already finished eating. I reflected that I should go to her room a little earlier starting tomorrow. If it was necessary to wake her up, I wish she had told me last night.

Princess Margaret barely ate any of her breakfast. I ate mine quickly.

"Now, we must go to class," says Princess Margaret, as elegant and laid-back as ever.

I broke out in a cold sweat this morning. As I walked up the stairs, I cast "Become clean!" using life magic, and I felt refreshed.

"Oh my, Patience. You have such a convenient magic. I'll be counting on you starting tomorrow."

Eh, so she intends not to wake up on her own again tomorrow, too? On top of that, she intends to make me help her get ready as well. I never knew being an attendant would be this exhausting! I resent Queen Victoria for this!
