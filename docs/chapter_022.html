What a total fool.

On Friday after school, I was called to the staff room by my homeroom teacher, Mr. Hagan.

"You passed the history test being returned on Monday. Therefore, Patience Granger, you are skipping ahead to the second year. From Monday, attend classes in the 2nd Year A class. Oh, and as for magical practicals, Mr. Jefferson has issued you a certificate of completion, so you are now exempt."

Huh? Wasn't I skipping grades because I wanted to be off on Mondays and Fridays?

"Mr. Hagan, please give me the schedule for the 2nd Year A class."

I received the schedule from Mr. Hagan and looked it over seriously.

"Huh, is it the same as the current one?"

"Obviously. All Primary A classes have the same schedule. Otherwise, students who skipped grades would be in trouble, wouldn't they? Once you reach the Secondary level, it becomes a credit-based system, so this consideration will no longer be necessary. Do you have any idea how difficult it is to coordinate schedules?"

I have no idea about such hardships.

"So, Classes B and C are also on the same schedule across all three grade levels, then?"

Mr. Hagan made a sour face.

"Only students in the A class skip grades. From the second year onwards, classes are divided by grades, you see. I'm having trouble with the parents of students complaining and making a fuss."

When classes are divided based on merit, the parents of high-ranking aristocrats who fall into Class B complain, huh? As I was listening to such gossip, I was struck by some shocking words.

"Oh, Patience. You can only skip a grade once a year. That’s unless you’ve received a certificate of completion like you did for practical magic."

"Ehh... I worked so hard because I wanted to go home... Teacher, I don't need to skip grades. I’m fine being a first-year. I already understand the content of the lessons, so I don't need to be at the academy, right?"

Mr. Hagan's thunder fell upon me.

"Don't say such foolish things! The grade-skipping system exists not only because taking classes you've already mastered is a waste of time, but also to further develop a student's abilities. Once you finish Primary school early and complete Secondary school, you could even go on to university."

In this world, girls marry young. The prime age is around 16 to 18. Therefore, parents don't send their daughters to university. But perhaps Mr. Hagan means that if I graduate from Secondary school early by skipping grades, I can learn without missing the prime marriage age. He doesn't know that there is no money for university at home. If there were, I would want to send the bright Nashius, and Henry too.

"That's right, let me introduce the homeroom teacher for 2nd Year A."

Since I was silent, Mr. Hagan perhaps remembered the circumstances of the Granger family and, feeling awkward, changed the subject.

"Mr. Kepner, this is Patience Granger, who will be skipping to the second year starting Monday."

Mr. Kepner was a slightly round gentleman.

"Oh, you're the Patience they're talking about in the staff room. I am Jungus Kepner. If you have any trouble after skipping to the second year, I'll be happy to help."

He seemed like a laid-back teacher, which was a relief.

"I am Patience Granger. Pleased to meet you. Um, I don't have the textbooks, what should I do?"

I received the 2nd-year textbooks and returned to my room.

"What a total fool I am!"

I dropped my textbooks onto the desk with a thud and sank down heavily. The plan to return home on Fridays and Mondays was impossible from the start.

"Next year I'll be in my third year; I wonder if I could skip a grade and enter the intermediate division? Oh no, I should have asked Professor Kepner about that."

If I could get into the intermediate division, I could graduate two years early. If by any chance I could skip a grade in my second year of the intermediate division, I might be able to graduate from the Royal Academy in three years. Since Nashius will be enrolling three years from now, leaving only Henry at home, I was worried about him. But before I start counting my chickens, I have to find out if skipping grades is even possible in the intermediate division. It seems to have a different system from the primary division. Professor Hagan did mention something about a credit system.

"And I want certificates of completion for mathematics, language arts, and history."

For classics, I'm currently relying on Patience, but for math, language arts, and history, it seems like I can manage the primary division if I study hard. Even if I can memorize everything for magic studies, I'm not sure if I'll reach the level required for a certificate of completion. I have no choice but to attend the classes for two years to find out.

"I'll do my best with classics and magic studies and aim for early graduation. Just wait for me, Nashius, Henry."

I don't actually dislike the Royal Academy that much. After all, Prince Keith, Ralph, and Hugo are all perfectly adorable to look at.

Prince Keith, for instance, has blonde hair and green eyes that make him look sassy; just looking at him is enough to make me happy.

Ralph-kun's mature act is also moe. With brown hair and blue eyes, he has well-defined features that suggest he'll grow up to be incredibly handsome.

Hugo-kun's slightly thoughtless, reckless side also makes my heart flutter. He's not quite at Ralph-kun's level, but if you look closely, he has well-defined features too. His red hair and blue eyes are cute.

I may be swooning over this shotacon paradise, but since they are Prince Keith and his entourage, I won't be getting close to them. I'm not a pervert, so I have no intention of laying a hand on ten-year-old boys! Besides, I have a real angel at home.

I even forgot that I was depressed and cheered up, thinking, "Tomorrow

I'll be able to see

my little brothers."
