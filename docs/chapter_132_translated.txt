Saturday study session

After lunch, we wait in the drawing room for Prince Keith to arrive. Well, it's awkward, isn't it? Samuel looks bored, too.

"Um, Auntie, may I practice the songs we'll use today on the Hanon?"

Aunt Liliana's eyebrows go up.

"My, Patience, haven't you practiced? That kind of lack of preparation is unacceptable. You should go upstairs and practice."

Samuel wanted to come along, but Auntie stopped him. She says he has to be there to greet Prince Keith when he arrives. I think it would be the same even if he were on the second floor, but I won't say that. Sorry, Samuel!

The nursery had become quite glitzy. The chairs, the desk, the level of opulence had definitely gone up. Auntie really tried her best.

I play a song in the Dane language on the Hanon. Yeah, I think I'll be fine with this much. I loved karaoke in my past life, so I'll try singing a little.

"So the Dane language really is similar to Classical."

You can tell well when you're singing it. Since I'm still a beginner at conversation, though. I'll look up some lyrics I don't quite understand in the Dane dictionary I brought from home. Wow, this is a pretty steamy love song. I play a few others and look up the lyrics too.

"Prince Keith has arrived."

Summoned by a servant, I hurriedly head downstairs.

"Prince Keith, thank you for coming to our home."

Count Northcourt and Auntie are offering greetings. Oh, Ralph and Hugo are here too, aren't they? They're greeting each other. I guess visits from the sons of noble families are welcomed.

"This time, I am inconveniencing Count Northcourt with my own selfishness. Now then, Patience, let's start the study session immediately."

Prince Keith cuts the pleasantries short and heads to the nursery for the study session. Ralph knows Classical, so I'll appoint him as an assistant. I'm not sure about Hugo. Well, he's Assistant 2.

"Prince Keith and Samuel, I want you to sing songs in the Dane language."

I hand them a songbook to share, and while I play the Hanon, they sing.

"Come on, Samuel and Prince Keith, please sing."

Ralph and Hugo, who are peeking from behind, sing too. After we sing a few times, we switch to a different song.

"You can kind of understand the content, can't you?"

Prince Keith and Samuel nod.

"Though, I don't think this will help me pass Classical..."

Prince Keith is skeptical. But Samuel seems to have grasped something. He tries reading the Classical textbook.

"I see, the Dane language is close to Classical. Patience, teach me more songs."

While teaching them a few difficult words, I have them sing. It seems Classical, which Prince Keith had a strong aversion to, has become much less painful for him.

"Songs are fine, but I want to learn the Dane language."

That's a bit difficult. I've only just started learning it myself.

"I have brought a textbook for simple greetings."

From then on, the five of us had an introductory Dane language lesson.

"I am Prince Keith."

"I am Samuel."

Ralph and Hugo are joining in and having fun, but it's almost tea time. I wonder if Eva is baking pancakes?

"Let's take a break for now," I suggest. Samuel was reluctant at first, but now he's happily practicing the Dane language with Prince Keith and the others.

"Oh, is it already that time?"

I head to the drawing room downstairs with Prince Keith and the others.

"Ah, that's pancakes!"

We are served tea, all prepared and ready.

"Ralph, Hugo, try some. These are delicious!"

The two of them don't usually touch the heavily sugared cakes, but they eat them at Prince Keith's recommendation.

"They are truly delicious."

Samuel likes sweets to begin with, so he polished off the pancakes. The whipped cream and strawberries vanished in an instant, too. But since he entered the academy and joined the horse riding club, he's been quite toned down—I wouldn't say he's thin, but he's not overweight, so it's fine. I'm just a shotacon who is weak for children.

Prince Keith thanks Aunt Liliana, who appeared to offer greetings.

"Thank you for allowing us to intrude upon your estate and for treating us to such delicious pancakes."

Good! Aunt Liliana should be relieved now. Properly entertaining guests is

the work of

a lady of the house, after all.

"Prince Keith, Samuel, what shall we do? I believe you understand the general approach now. All that remains is for each of you to study on your own."

Prince Keith thinks for a moment before answering.

"While I no longer feel the same aversion to Classical as I did before, I don't know if I can skip a grade with this alone. Patience, if I can skip a grade in Classical, I'll be in the third year. Could you teach me a little more?"

Can't Ralph do that? I glance at Ralph, but he declines. So he’s been teaching them countless times already, then.

"I want you to teach me a little more, too. And I’d like you to teach me a bit more math as well."

Samuel is asking me, too. The time I have with my younger brothers is just dwindling away.

"Lady Patience, perhaps you are suited to be a teacher?"

Ralph paid me a compliment, but I’ll pass on being praised to death.

"Countess Northcourt, would it be alright if we came to disturb you again next Saturday?"

Ah, Prince Keith, that’s underhanded. Auntie would never say no.

"Of course. Please, feel free to come to our home anytime."

Good grief, my Saturday next week is ruined too. How sad. If it’s going to be like this, I’m going to work you all hard.

"Then, let's work hard and learn grammar for another hour!"

Since you've interrupted my time with

my younger brothers, I'll make sure you study until you can definitely skip a grade!

"Patience is kind of scary."

Of course I am! Welcome to hell!

I have Prince Keith read the Classical textbook aloud. He was reluctant at first, but then he seemed to have a flash of insight.

"Wait, Classical is just an old literary style of Laurence, isn't it? It's a little different from what we use now, and Dane is closer, but the origin is the same."

Exactly. Even children like Patience and Nashius were reading it fluently. It was just because I was rejecting it, saying "I hate it!", that I didn't understand.

"That's why you only need to memorize the grammar that's different from the modern Laurence language in the textbook. After that, just keep reading Classical and your reading comprehension will improve."

Samuel has a genius-level ear for music, so he's surprised that he can understand the Classical textbook just by reading it aloud.

"There are still parts I don't understand, but I can grasp the content of most books. At this rate, it doesn't look like I'll fail Classical."

That’s a relief. Can I leave them to self-study now? That was naive of me.

"Patience, teach me math too," Samuel starts saying, and Prince Keith begins to greedily talk about wanting to receive a completion certificate.

"Please do that on your own," I try to push them away.

"The Countess Northcourt said we could come anytime, you know."

Thanks to Auntie, I couldn't escape. *Sniffle!*
