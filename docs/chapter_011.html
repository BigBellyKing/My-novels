Everyday magic is convenient! But be careful not to overdo it!

I’ll do a little bit of cleaning and repairing using everyday magic at a time. If I try to do too much all at once, I’ll get tired. It must be that using too much magical power is bad for me. I could look it up in a book, but I’ll prioritize improving my daily life first. Besides, I’ll be taught how to do it once I go to the academy anyway.

"My sister, you will be enrolling in the academy in January, won't you? It is a joyous occasion, but I will miss you."

While I was teaching Nashius in the morning, he dropped a bombshell. It’s December now. In other words, there is only one month left.

"The Royal Academy is in Romano, so can't I just commute? So, you won't have to be lonely."

Nashius looks surprised. His perfectly round gray eyes are cute.

"But you said before that you couldn't borrow a horse every day, so you would be staying in the dormitory."

Hmm? I search Patience's memories. The Royal Academy is near the royal palace, and furthermore, it’s on the opposite side from the Granger house. Of course, I could commute by carriage, but... on the days I went to church, I had my own carriage, but it seems I rented the horse for half a day. Poverty is sad.

"That was true. But I will come home on my days off."

It’s not a distance that can’t be walked. If it’s to see my younger brothers, I’ll walk. Or a hackney carriage? I know they exist, but Patience has never ridden one. It costs money, so I guess I’ll be walking.

"But it is impossible for you to go alone, Sister."

Ah, right, a young lady isn't allowed to walk alone without a maid.

"Nashius, I'll find a way. Believe in your sister."

Where there's a will, there's a way! Though having said that, it’s painful not having the funds to back it up. Besides, after I’m gone, who will oversee the younger brothers' studies? Ah, there’s Father, I suppose.

Aside from the studies, I really want to improve my living conditions! I thought, but then I was caught by Mary.

"Young Lady, what did you do to these curtains and this carpet?"

Cleaning the nursery is Mary's job. She noticed right away that they were as good as new.

"It's everyday magic."

When I answered that, Mary dashed off, and before I knew it, she had returned with a mountain of clothes.

"These are uniforms for the Royal Academy. Can you use your everyday magic to clean them?"

There are many hand-me-down uniforms from a female relative, all in different sizes.

"It makes me tired if I use it all at once, so I'll try with the current size first."

I chant "Become clean!" at the smallest sized uniform. Uniforms get worn out quite a bit since you wear them every day, you know? Like the cuffs or the elbows getting shiny. They became as good as new. Ah, I'm a little tired.

"My, with this, I won't have to be embarrassed. I had been thinking about cutting pieces from the larger uniforms to repair the ones that were too frayed, or perhaps unraveling the larger ones to sew them back together."

A gray dress and a jacket. Plus, it has the Royal Academy emblem on it. It’s fairly ordinary. Just as I was thinking that so nonchalantly, more and more were brought to me.

While I was wondering, "How many pairs of socks do I need?", I suddenly thought of a business repairing silk socks worn by nobles. Yes, I remember reading in novels from the Taisho era and such about using darning stockings as a side job.

"Hey, Mary. I'm dexterous. I want to put that to good use."

Somehow, I feel this mending is different from standard Life Magic, so I insist that it's sewing. I looked up "Beginner Life Magic," but there was nothing about mending.

"But, my lady..."

Mary was worried. She was torn between her enthusiasm to prepare outfits befitting a viscount's daughter for my entry into the dormitory, and the conflict over whether it was right to let a young lady do piecework.

"I know that you sometimes do sewing piecework."

Yes, Patience also knew that Mary was doing piecework. Just how poor is the Granger family! That included darning socks, too. After all, silk socks are expensive, and it's a waste to throw them away just because they snagged a little. Even for nobles. While they might wear new ones for parties and such, since they are hidden by clothes usually, it seems to be common practice to wear them even if they have been darned.

"But, if they become like new, people will be suspicious."

Mary also looks worried, wondering if my Life Magic is a bit different. It doesn't seem to be the same as my mother Julianne's, either. Is this a cheat?

"But, I've heard that master darners can make the stitches invisible."

"That is indeed true, but mine still show the stitches a little."

Mary has caved in quite a bit. Undergarments and such should be new, right? Even if I could make old things look like new, I couldn't bring myself to do that. Socks are barely acceptable, I suppose.

"We can just say that your skills have improved. If we do that, we can prepare enough supplies. We might even be able to get a new coat."

I tempted Mary and secured the piecework of darning socks.

But, piecework doesn't pay well. It was the same in another world.

5 teems for 1 pair. 50 teems for 10 pairs. In other words, you have to darn 20 pairs just to get 1 lohm. Earning even one silver coin is hard work. You have to darn 200 pairs to get a gold coin.

"Ah, I feel dizzy."

Fighting off the dizziness, thanks to the darning, I managed to get underwear and a coat.

Life Magic is convenient, but you have to be careful not to overuse it.
