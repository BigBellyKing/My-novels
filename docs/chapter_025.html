Lady-in-waiting to Princess Margaret

Time flies when you're having fun. Nashius praised me so much for skipping a grade. It made me so happy. Henry didn't seem to fully understand what skipping a grade meant, but he praised me by saying, "My big sister is smart, isn't she!" He just repeated what Nashius said, but he’s so cute I could kiss him.

It's already Sunday afternoon. That means I have to go back to the dormitory. I board the carriage with a heavy heart, longing to stay.

"My lady, it is wonderful that you are to be Princess Margaret's lady-in-waiting."

Mary was already beside herself with excitement.

"I'm worried whether I'll be able to fulfill the duty." This was my honest feeling.

"You will be fine, my lady."

Right, if it were Patience, she would have been fine. But Patience is dead. Though for being dead, she sure is noisy about this and that.

Even though I didn't want to go back to the dorm, the carriage arrived heartlessly.

"My lady, you must go and greet Princess Margaret."

Even though I didn't want to become a lady-in-waiting, being lectured by Mary made me dislike it even more.

"Yes, I know."

I intended to send Mary away and spend the night quietly reading second-year textbooks. But she wouldn't back down.

"Do you know where her room is? I'll go ask the dormitory warden. In the meantime, please write a letter asking if you may visit her room."

If I were a lady-in-waiting, I wouldn't need permission in advance to visit her room, but Mary cautioned me about various things, saying she might not even know my face yet.

The elegant-looking stationery set Mary bought because she said I needed it. Sighing, wondering how many pairs of socks that could have mended, I left it to Patience to write the letter requesting permission for the visit.

"The room is Special Room 1 on the third floor. I will go deliver your letter."

Although she mostly does menial work, Mary is a lady's maid who came with my mother Julianne from her parents' home upon her marriage. She is as lively as a fish in water, performing her true duties.

"My lady, she says you are to come now."

Sigh—they say sighing lets happiness escape, but sometimes you just can't get through it without letting one out. Mary left with eyes full of expectation.

Special Room 1—even the door and the room number plate were gorgeous. I didn't want to knock, but Patience was being annoying, insisting that making someone wait was a breach of etiquette. I knocked as elegantly as possible.

With a click, a maid opened the door for me.

"I am Patience Granger."

"Please come in, she is waiting for you."

If there’s already a maid, I don't see why she would need a lady-in-waiting.

The special room seemed to consist of two connecting rooms. There was no bed in the room I entered, so it was likely in the room beyond. Princess Margaret was sitting on a sofa much more luxurious than the dormitory-provided furniture.

Her platinum blonde hair and green eyes are just like Prince Keith's. She is a beautiful princess.

"I am Patience Granger."

I curtsied, holding the hem of my uniform skirt.

"So, you are the Patience my mother chose to be a lady-in-waiting."
