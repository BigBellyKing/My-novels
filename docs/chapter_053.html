Sleeping Beauty Princess Margaret

"Patience, let's ride together."

I couldn't possibly refuse since the Queen herself had given permission. During the carriage ride for four, Lady-in-waiting Charlotte and Mary didn't open their mouths. In other words, only Princess Margaret and I were having a conversation.

"I'm so glad you came, Patience. Mother is in a very bad mood. Being in the same carriage as her is suffocating."

Princess Margaret's hair, as she complained, could hardly be called well-groomed. It was tied back with a ribbon, but the ends were still messy from sleep. There should be plenty of ladies-in-waiting and maids in the royal palace, though.

"Don't tell me, did you oversleep?"

She untied the ribbon herself and shoved her hair toward me, demanding I do something about it.

"Because the palace maids don't wake me up the way you do, Patience. They wake me up poorly, and then I'm the one who gets scolded by Mother."

It seems I’ve spoiled her. I must reflect on this, but first things first: her hair. It must be properly groomed before lunch.

The Queen had given her permission, saying it couldn't be helped if she wanted to ride with her lady-in-waiting. I understand the meaning behind those words.

"Mary, do you have a brush?"

Mary, the model maid, had a brush in her handbag. In truth, Mary is better at styling hair, but that would crush the pride of the palace maids. Patience had given permission for the Princess's lady-in-waiting to do it, so that was fine.

I chanted, "Become beautiful!" using life magic, and the bedhead smoothed out, leaving her hair glossy.

"I can only do a simple hairstyle."

I used the brush to pull it into a half-up style and secured it with a ribbon. Compared to this, my own hairstyle was flashier. That was Mary’s doing, as she had been quite eager.

I wrapped the loose hair around my fingertips and chanted "Become curls" using life magic several times. It turned into curled hair like my school friend Catherine’s. It wasn't quite a full-blown ringlet, but I think it looks nice.

"Is there a mirror?"

Mary silently handed me a mirror. I then passed it to Princess Margaret. Apparently, it is not acceptable for my maid to hand it directly to her. What a hassle.

"Oh, how lovely. From now on, style my hair like this."

"If there is enough time, I shall."

Assuming you don't wake up at the very last minute, that is.

"Say, where are we having lunch?"

She directed her question to Charlotte, the lady-in-waiting. It seems she’s allowed to speak at times like this.

"We are scheduled to have lunch at the Laforet Duke's estate, Your Highness."

That’s a name I’ve heard somewhere before.

"Oh, lunch at the estate of Albert the Eccentric? Patience, be careful. The Duke of Laforet is even more eccentric than his son. You must never leave my side."

Mary's face went pale.

"Lord Albert is simply a man of deep musical passion, to the point of being the deputy head of the music club," I said to reassure her.

"Oh, that goes beyond love for music. Patience, if you’re that carefree, you’ll end up locked away in the Laforet estate. The Duke of Laforet was the one standing out with his loud applause at the new music recital, wasn't he?"

Ah, that was the guardian who had stood up in excitement. Come to think of it, he was wearing quite luxurious attire.

"Understood. I will not leave Princess Margaret's side."

I hope we arrive before Princess Margaret, who skipped breakfast, gets into a bad mood.

"It is so boring. If I could play the lute at a time like this, it would take my mind off things. Patience, tutors for Jane and Marcus are also accompanying us to the summer villa. You are good at Hanon, but you cannot play the lute. You should learn from a music teacher."

I thought that much might be fine, but things were added one after another.

"You also said you were bad at dancing, right? Since you are good at music, you should have a good sense of rhythm. You danced well at the Green Leaf Festival, too. If you improve a little more, you should be able to get a certificate of completion."

I am a fool for thinking that if I could also get a certificate of completion for dancing, my free time would increase.

"I received my dance completion certificate in the spring semester, so if you get an exemption, we can enjoy music during that time."

I would like to be spared from that.

"I have to skip a grade to the intermediate division, so I want to devote that time to studying."

I was completely ignored.

"What are you talking about? You have already received a certificate of completion for mathematics, and you are almost exempt from practical skills, right? You should be able to spend time with me in music as a lady-in-waiting for at least the time I am exempt."

It seems that special dance training has been decided.

"Ah, come to think of it, Keith said he couldn't catch a horse. Surely it's not that you can't ride a horse, is it?"

Prince Keith, you! Why say such unnecessary things.

"I have never ridden a horse. I have never even thought of wanting to."

I refused flatly. But it was useless.

"Well, if you can't ride, you'll be in trouble when you're invited to a hunt, won't you? You must learn to ride during the summer vacation."

I can't say, "I won't be invited to a hunt, and even if I were, I don't have a horse, so I can't go." I've never even ridden a horse in my previous life. I just hope I don't fall off and die.

Lady-in-waiting Charlotte and Mary kept their mouths shut while listening to Princess Margaret's harassment born of hunger.

Me? I was only half-listening. I'll probably be made to practice the lute and dance, but I think I'll only do a little bit of horseback riding. Because Princess Margaret, who is overflowing with love for music, can be bribed with things like duet pieces by Hanon. I wonder if I remember Mozart's Sonata for Two Pianos?
