Home

"Let's bake sweets!"

The stable hand from the royal carriage had courteously delivered them to the mansion. Even at a time like this, the butler, Wyatt, handled it calmly.

"Welcome home, Milady."

He opened the door and welcomed me just as he always did.

"Wyatt, please take the basket from Sophie and hand it to Eva."

A young lady of the house would never carry a basket to the kitchen herself. Before Sophie could hand over the basket and make sure I boarded the carriage, I had already hugged my younger brothers. I don't want to be apart from them anymore.

During the short time before dinner, I listened to stories of the days we hadn't met.

"Sister, we can use the toilet now. I never knew there was a toilet in the mansion until now."

Wyatt had bought magic stones first. Naturally, improving our standard of living should come first. Besides, eight-year-old Nashius must have felt embarrassed using a chamber pot by now. He looks happy.

"And I started sword practice with George."

"Henry, stop swinging that wooden sword around in your room."

The wooden sword for children was likely handmade by George. He must have made it from firewood. Once they get a bit bigger, they'll need more solid wooden swords, but for now, this is enough.

If George is freed from washing chamber pots, he should have time to accompany them in sword practice.

"Next are horses and Hanon."

I don't have paints for art, but I can teach them how to sketch. I can teach them at least the steps for dancing. Since horses need feed if you keep them, it's unavoidable that they are put on the back burner. I'll indirectly ask Wyatt to acquire an old Hanon piano book.

"Milady, returning in a royal carriage means you are performing your duties as Princess Margaret's attendant splendidly. Even so, why would they give you eggs and butter?"

She was brushing my hair, but I suddenly remembered that I had forgotten about the basket. When I see my brothers, everything else becomes secondary.

"I received those from the Queen. I wanted to bake the sweets I used to make with Mother for my brothers."

Mary’s hands stopped.

"You met the Queen?"

"Yes, she said she wanted to see the face of Princess Margaret's attendant."

Mary’s hands were trembling with emotion, but she finished fixing my hair. Dinner was a bit more plentiful than before. Since vegetables can be harvested in the greenhouse, the side dishes have increased. Even with my sudden return, seeing this amount makes me feel relieved, thinking that they must be eating the same way every day. Come to think of it,

My brothers

were also a bit plumper. They're even better to hug now. Hehe.

Saturday morning is for studying with my brothers. Plus, we do maintenance on the greenhouse vegetables together. Nashius needs exercise, too, you know.

"Let's bake sweets!" Just as lunch ended and the servants finished eating, I headed for the semi-basement kitchen, but I was caught by Mary in the maid's room on the way.

"Milady, the kitchen is no place for you..." she started, trying to stop me, but I have the Queen on my side.

"I promised the Queen I would feed my brothers the sweets I made with Mother. Therefore, I must fulfill that promise."

"The Queen is..." I enter the kitchen while Mary is still stunned. The kitchen is unnecessarily large as well. It is too big for cooking for a family of four and four servants.

"Eva, the Queen gave us eggs, butter, sugar, and fresh cream. I’m going to make the snacks Mother taught me for my younger brothers."

That said, Patience was also young at the time, so her memory is hazy. She only has a vague recollection of baking something like a thin pancake or griddle cake.

"I'm going to make pancakes."

Since I am not used to the cooking utensils of this different world, I will start with pancakes, which are simple and remain in my memory.

"Young Miss, what is a pancake? And how do you make it?"

I cannot ignore Eva, the cook. I have caused her so much trouble. Besides, I need her to keep recreating the sweets from my memories.

"You just beat the eggs, add milk, mix in sugar and flour, and bake it in a frying pan."

Right, I don't even know where the tools are.

In the end, I end up baking the pancakes while giving instructions to Eva.

"I wonder if there is any baking powder? If not, we can whip the eggs... do we have a whisk?"

"A whisk, we used to have one, but..."

Eva shakes her head sadly, saying they haven't bought one since it broke.

"It's fine. I’ll whip it using life magic."

In an instant, the egg whites become fluffy and foamy.

"My, what a convenient magic."

Indeed, making sweets often requires whipping. Life magic is quite useful.

"Put a piece of butter in the frying pan, and once it melts, add one ladleful of the pancake batter."

Mary stops me from handling the fire in case I get burned, so I have Eva do the baking while I give instructions.

"Once the surface is covered in little bubbles, flip it over."

A lovely aroma fills the kitchen.

"Since we don't have butter and honey for this, sprinkle a little sugar on top and it's done. You could also serve it with some whipped fresh cream. Let's have some tea."

I whip some fresh cream and hand it to Eva. The custom of having tea was cut back in the Granger household to save money, but I want to revive it today.

I have Wyatt tell Father that we are having tea.

"If we are having tea, the drawing room would be more appropriate than the dining room. I shall go light the fireplace."

It’s a good thing the drawing room was also repaired. The four of us eat the pancakes and drink thin tea.

"Big sister, this is very delicious."

"Yeah, it’s good."

"Henry, you’ve got fresh cream on your cheek." I wipe it off for him.

Father is also eating it with delight. It’s a happy family gathering.
