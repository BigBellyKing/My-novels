At the Summer Palace, during dinner

I head to my room, hurried along by Mary.

"Oh, I can see the ocean from the room too!"

As I gaze at the sea through the open window, Mary rushes me into the bath.

"You must change before dinner. Please hurry."

That's right, we change for dinner at the Granger house, too. It's only natural to change at the Summer Palace as well. Thanks to life magic, drying my hair is instantaneous.

It's just a single word: "Dry!"

"Please wear this."

I didn't recognize the dress Mary held out. It was a dress prepared for me by the Queen.

"It looks like such a high-end dress. But wouldn't it be better to wear it on a day when His Majesty the King is here?"

Mary hurries me, saying that I can just wear it again that day.

"It would be rude not to wear it after it was given to you."

In my previous life, I didn't wear pink very often. If anything, I mostly wore monotone, with maybe a splash of blue.

"It suits you well."

Mary is quite biased. But, I think it suits Patience. I'm a little less skinny than in winter, after all. I think I look quite cute, don't you?

I don't want to gain excess fat, but I would like to gain a little more muscle. I'll try my best at swimming. Princess Margaret, I wonder if you could forget about the horseback riding?

Hurried along by Mary, I entered the room before the dining hall, but no one was there.

"Geez, I'm way too early!"

I wanted to enjoy the palace bath a bit more leisurely. Just as I was complaining to myself, Prince Keith and Prince Richard arrived. Heh, they look pretty cool. As expected of princes!

Why is Prince Keith blushing so much?

"Patience, you actually look decent enough when you dress like that."

I had just thought he looked cool, but he's just a child at heart. Well, I'm a shotacon, so I find such childishness endearing too.

"Keith, that is rude."

Look, Prince Richard has stepped in to scold him.

"No, I understand that Prince Keith was complimenting me."

I want to avoid Prince Richard getting into a bad mood before dinner. I followed up properly, but Prince Keith is still grumbling. Geez, if he doesn't stop that, he'll keep making his beloved older brother angry.

Oh, Princess Margaret entered the room at just the right time. Prince Richard and Prince Keith stood up to greet her.

See, Prince Keith can be polite too. If he just kept that up in front of Prince Richard, he wouldn't make him angry as often.

"Princess Margaret, you look very beautiful."

Princess Margaret, who liked her curled hair, had it beautifully styled by her maid. She wore a dress that suited her green eyes, which also had a long hem. That's right, she's turning thirteen already. Her daytime attire is knee-length, but her dressed-up evening appearance is already completely that of a lady.

"Thank you, it suits you too, Patience."

Princess Margaret follows the rules of female society, too. If you are complimented, it is the rule to return the compliment.

"It is a dress given to me by the Queen. I am very happy."

Well, I am happy about the dress. But I would be happier spending time with my younger brothers at home. That is something I must not say out loud.

The Queen has arrived. Prince Richard and Prince Keith stand up, as do Princess Margaret and I.

"Now, let's eat."

His Majesty is not here tonight, so Prince Richard is acting as escort. Prince Keith is with Princess Margaret, and I am fine on my own.

Dinners are nerve-wracking, aren't they? Even though I eat with

the High-Class Dining Hall

every day. It’s the dressing up, and the presence of Queen Victoria that makes a big difference.

"I heard from Margaret that you like fish, Patience."

"Yes, I don't have many opportunities to eat fish in Romano, so I am very happy to have been invited to the Summer Palace."

Ah, Prince Keith looks like he's about to say he hates fish or something. Yeah, lately, Prince Keith's "gaffe-o-meter" has started to work.

"Your Majesty, thank you for this dress."

Please, just keep your mouth shut. I can manage Prince Richard's mood, but don't anger the Queen. At least, not in front of me.

"Patience, it suits you very well."

With that, my conversation for the dinner is over. Now, I'll just focus on eating.

The appetizer was—wow—a jelly terrine of shrimp and something like sea urchin. It's delicious. This is sea urchin, right? Even when I search Patience's memories, the name doesn't come up. I guess she’s never eaten it before.

"Patience, you really eat like you're enjoying it."

I was complimented by the Queen, wasn't I? I wasn't being looked down upon, right?

"Yes, it is my first time eating it, and I didn't know such a delicious thing existed."

Prince Keith happily tells me the name. He loves acting superior, doesn't he? He is just a child, after all.

"This pink one is a Caleb shrimp. The orange, fuzzy stuff is horse-dung sea urchin. The name is terrible, but..."

As expected, Prince Keith noticed Queen Victoria's stern gaze and closed his mouth. "Horse-dung sea urchin"—is it the same name as in my previous life, or is it just being translated by the language of this other world? I'm not thinking about that. I just love shrimp and sea urchin so much. I even want to eat the sea urchin that Prince Keith left behind.

The seafood soup and the fish poêlé were also delicious. They were on the menu at

the High-Class Dining Hall

a few times in winter, but as it got warmer, they disappeared. Romano is inland, after all. Prince Keith left both his soup and fish uneaten. Good grief.

It was a delicious dinner, but the dessert is still a bit underwhelming. But there's fruit, so I'll eat that instead.

"Normally, we would leave the gentlemen here and head to the salon alone, but let's all go together."

Oh no, there's a harp in the salon, isn't there? I only have a bad feeling about this.
