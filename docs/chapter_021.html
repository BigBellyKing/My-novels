I’m going to skip a grade!

Happy New Year.

I look forward to your continued support this year.

I want to avoid being in the same grade as Prince Keith, and I don't like being viewed as a rival by Louise, so I'm seriously considering skipping a grade.

It’s time for the problematic magic practical exam. Even if I fail art or music later, I can still skip a grade.

There's actually a magic practical room. Well, it would be dangerous if fireballs were flying around inside the academy. It looks like a large gymnasium, but the floor is still dirt. I’m sure it’s good for using earth magic and the like. But I wonder, would I use life magic here? I’m getting worried.

There are students from other grades, but for some reason, the first-years are sticking together. Huh, a fireball heading toward a target! Is that what a fireball is? It's my first time seeing one. I’ve seen them in games and anime, but it's quite impressive to see it in person. I don’t want to get hit by that. Oh, they wanted to show off to the lowerclassmen, I see. How cute. Ah, they're being scolded by a scary-looking teacher. Well, that’s to be expected.

"All students in 1st Year Class A, gather here!"

The one scolding the upperclassmen was Mr. Shepherd, the magic practical teacher, who looked more like a jock than a wizard. He seems like he’d be a hot-blooded instructor. Will he even recognize the value of life magic?

"Everyone, split up according to your specialty magic."

Fire, water, wind, earth, light, and then physical enhancement and life magic. Besides me, there were two other girls. If I remember correctly, they were Lucienne and Caitlin. They are all daughters of viscounts, but unlike my house, it seems they possess territories. Life magic feels a bit isolating, so we huddled together at the edge of the practical room. Teachers began to enter the room one after another.

"Have your respective teachers look at your elementary techniques."

As expected, Mr. Shepherd seems to be in charge of physical enhancement instruction. But he seems to be teaching fire magic as well. Prince Keith, my condolences.

Louise is the only one for light. But they’ve gone out of their way to call in a monk. I can tell expectations are high.

"So, these three are the life magic students, huh? I am Angela Jefferson. If you master life magic, you can do anything."

Ms. Jefferson was a slightly elderly noblewoman. She had white hair but was vigorous. She’s an energetic grandmotherly teacher. We were all relieved. Somehow, life magic feels like it’s looked down upon more than other magics, so hearing her say that we could do anything made us happy.

With this teacher, maybe I won't have to chant those embarrassing incantations. Even if I were ten years old, being twenty-five mentally, it's embarrassing to say chuunibyou-like incantations.

The seating is also by social standing, so the practical exams start with Lucienne. It seems there are differences in the size and history of territories even among the same viscount families. Patience seems knowledgeable, but I’m not interested.

Oh, she’s chanting an embarrassing incantation. But, it doesn't look like her body got much cleaner, right? Lucienne isn't exactly dirty to begin with, so it's hard to tell. Caitlin didn’t receive a passing grade from Ms. Jefferson either, but it was good that she seemed to enjoy the magic practical.

Finally, it’s my turn.

"Become clean!" I chant as usual. I feel refreshed. Since I’m not dirty either, it probably doesn't look like much has changed, but it makes me feel refreshed too. So convenient, so convenient!

"Well, Patience Granger. There is no need for me to teach you. The practical magic examination is already over."

Grandma teacher? Did I pass?

Professor Jefferson called over Professor Shepard, who was busy providing passionate instruction, and handed him a piece of paper.

"Eh, Professor Jefferson. This student is still a first-year, you know."

Something is strange. Isn't this skipping a grade?

"Yes, because there is nothing left for me to teach. I will issue a certificate of completion."

Wait a second! I don't want to stand out. Skipping a grade is also conspicuous, but I wanted to do that for various reasons, so that's fine. Grandma teacher, please give me a break. Ah, there's Louise's gaze on me again... I just have to be glad that it looks like I'll be skipping a grade.

I also passed the magic theory test on Friday. Many people passed this one. The textbook was thin, so you just had to memorize it. Perhaps because Prince Keith passed, Ralph and Hugo did too, and Louise as well. I have a feeling that even if I skip a grade, I'll have quite a few classes with Prince Keith and Louise.

Pulling myself together, next is art. I like to draw. Patience couldn't buy paints and such, but she was good at sketching. Her skill might even be better than my drawing. I passed here along with Louise too. Sigh.

For music, you have to play an instrument. Ah, something piano-like seems to be called a "Hanon." Formally, it seems the name changes depending on the size, but it's all collectively called Hanon.

The sound is a high-pitched one, closer to a harpsichord than a piano. I had Patience do her best here and she passed. Many girls passed music. Of course, Louise did too. Sigh.

Prince Keith also played a string instrument that looked like a lute and passed. A lute, perhaps? As expected, when Prince Keith passes, Ralph passes too. He was playing a flute-like pipe. However, unfortunately, Hugo failed. He played the same lute as Prince Keith, but he didn't seem used to it. Wouldn't it have been better to choose an instrument he was good at?

"Doesn't Patience have a perfect pass record?"

I got glared at by Prince Keith. He's the type who gets angry when someone has more passing marks than him, isn't he? That's why Ralph keeps a low profile. But is that really okay? I feel like something is wrong, but I think I'll avoid getting involved with Prince Keith.
