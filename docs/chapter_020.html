"I hate that the entrance ceremony is on a Monday. It’s terrible that I won’t be able to see my little brothers for an entire week."

Well, complaining to the calendar won't help. Tuesday consists of Classics, Mathematics, Magic Studies, and Dance. I’m relieved that failing Dance doesn't seem to affect grade skipping.

After all, the Granger family doesn't have a tutor to teach us how to dance. Plus, we don't have the money to host a dance party, nor have I ever been invited to one. Maybe I’m not invited because Patience is still young, but don't they have birthday parties for girls among relatives? Don't children dance with each other?

I used to like dancing. When I was a university student, I even went to clubs. But this isn't like noble dancing, is it? I wish I had joined the ballroom dancing club. I was quite interested in it, but back then I was young and felt embarrassed to dance as a pair with a man. Wait, 10-year-old boys and girls dancing? I have a bad feeling about this.

As a result, Classics was a breeze thanks to Patience. Mathematics was a breeze even for me. For lunch, I finished eating quickly, returned to the dormitory, and read through the Magic Studies textbook one more time in my room.

In Japanese, quite a few people passed. I mean, it was easy. Prince Keith, Ralph, Hugo, and also Louise passed. It seems several other girls passed as well.

The history test was a joint effort between Patience and me, but I think we passed. There was only one question where the date was vague, so I relied on Patience's memory.

The written tests are over. All that's left is the practical exam.
