Home

If it doesn't exist, I'll just make it!

To get the ham that Eva brings home, I need money.

"I want money!"

Even if I phrase it elegantly, the words remain heartless.

"I need protein!"

It doesn't have to be expensive ham. Meat or fish would work, but my memory of Patience suggests that eggs are a luxury item, so that’s probably out.

The royal capital, Romano, is located inland. In other words, the only fish available are river fish, and dried fish aren't very common either. Therefore, I figured the only way to feed my growing younger brothers protein would be with beans.

"I think we could grow beans in the garden, but it's winter..."

Something in Patience's memory clicked. A greenhouse!

Since Mary keeps my new coat until it's time to go to school, I wear my tight coat and walk around the garden.

"Ah, when Mother was alive, she used to grow roses in the greenhouse even in winter..."

Patience's sentimentality brought up images of the greenhouse and roses from days gone by, but now the glass is shattered, leaving only the skeleton. Looking at the ruins of the greenhouse, I feel a flutter of excitement at the hope that if I can fix this, maybe I can grow vegetables.

"I don't need roses. You can't eat them."

The Patience in my head is fussing, saying "Roses are lovely," but food is more important right now.

"I don't want my brothers to starve!" Patience fell silent. Patience, who has a bit of a brother complex, is weak when it comes to her younger brothers.

I'll have Mary buy winter vegetable seedlings and seeds, as well as beans, even though they're usually planted in the summer. In the meantime, I’ll work on fixing the greenhouse.

"Glass, be restored!"

I don't need roses, I wish intensely, and cast daily magic.

"Oh, the greenhouse is restored. Now for the inside."

The inside of the greenhouse is warm. That's to be expected, of course. But the remnants of the withered roses remained. I can't plant vegetables like this.

"It's impossible to plow with a hoe. I can't ask any more of George."

I wasn't sure if I could do it, but I placed my hands on the dirt and chanted, "Rose remains, turn to dust!"

With a rustle, the withered rose bushes turned into dust and fell to the ground.

"Daily magic really can do anything, huh?"

A poor person has no time to seriously wonder if this might be a cheat ability. I have to make the planting rows before Mary gets back. I'm an office worker raised in the city, which means I have no agricultural experience. The most I've done is digging for sweet potatoes in a class in elementary school. But, as common sense goes, I know that if you want to plant something, you need rows.

"Become planting rows!" Oh well, here goes. I chanted it, and it turned into rows.

"Thank goodness. Getting calluses on my hands isn't very lady-like."

Patience's common sense is quite invasive. This is troublesome. I left planting the seeds and seedlings to Mary. I gave up on doing it myself because she absolutely wouldn't let me. By the way, she said it would be fine if it were roses or flowers. It seems I will have to take Mary's standards into account when improving our living conditions from now on.

The day after I planted the seedlings, full of ulterior motives and thinking how nice it would be if it were a "cheat festival," I chanted, "Grow big." Just like a scene from an anime, the seedlings grew, and sprouts emerged from the seeds.

『This is doing whatever I want. So this is life magic!』

In this way, the prospects for growing vegetables were set, and I was ready to discuss things with Eva.

「How should I tell her?」

Because my house is pathetic, I have the servants doing side jobs or sending them out to work. I can't blame them for it, but I think it's wrong. I want to make her stop working at the market without blaming Eva. And that’s coming from a 10-year-old child. It's difficult. While I was agonizing in my room, I heard a modest knock.

「My Lady, it is Wyatt. Do you have a moment?」

I don't recall the butler, Wyatt, ever coming to my room. What could it be? By any chance, has my strange life magic been discovered? Well, it would be found out, wouldn't it? Nashius seemed to realize something was strange right away, and Mary figured it out immediately, too. I think the only ones who don't know are little Henry and my father.

「Yes, come in.」

My Lady speaks in a commanding tone to the servants. I wonder if Wyatt, who is older, thinks I'm acting pompous.

Wyatt, whose brown hair—which had more gray strands than my father's—was tied back without a single strand out of place, entered the room and bowed respectfully.

「My Lady, thank you for your time.」

My heart was pounding wondering what he would say, but I smiled elegantly. I have to see how he plays his hand!

「I am very happy that the mansion has become so clean lately. Furthermore, I was surprised that the greenhouse has been tended to and vegetables are growing,」 he said, looking as if he wasn't surprised in the slightest.

「Regarding Eva's matter, I leave it to you.」

So, I decided to push the problem onto him as well. After all, it's better for the butler to say it than for me to say it.

「I apologize for causing you concern over such a trivial matter. In regards to that...」

I really am no match for Wyatt. He accepted the matter of Eva, but it seems that just having vegetables and beans on the dining table isn't good enough. To raise funds to buy meat, I was asked to repair some broken antiques. Well, this will improve the meals for my younger brothers, and on top of that, it seems the rental fee for the carriage horse I take home on weekends has also been secured. Good grief, he’s quite the capable butler. It’s a mystery why he’s in such a poor viscount’s family.
