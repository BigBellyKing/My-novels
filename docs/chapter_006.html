Preparing to go to the church

"Patience, come in."

The study had a calm atmosphere, and I could understand the desire to shut oneself away in here. It was like a man’s sanctuary, the perfect space for escaping reality.

"Father, did you have some business with me?"

It was translated by Patience into a refined, elegant question.

"Sit there. I think it’s about time you were examined at the church, too."

Gah... Did my possession get found out? Am I going to be exorcised? I turned pale.

"There’s no need to be so nervous. Checking for magical aptitude is simple. I’m sure you can use daily life magic, Patience. Julianne was quite good at it, too. It’s best to have it checked before you start school."

I felt relieved at my father’s beaming smile, but... daily life magic? Patience! Tell me about it.

"What is 'daily life magic'?"

I intended to 'google' Patience’s memories, but it seems I had spoken aloud.

"Oh my, daily life magic is magic used for daily living. Many people can use it, but Julianne was especially skilled at it."

Patience’s memories were more helpful than my father’s explanation, which I felt I understood but didn't quite grasp.

Daily life magic seemed to be quite convenient magic for things like lighting fires, conjuring water, turning on lights, cleaning, and keeping the body clean.

It's the classic magic trope!

Reincarnated into a world of swords and magic! It sounded like an exciting adventure story, but although I’m a noble, I’m poor, and it seems many commoners can use daily life magic. If anything, it’s magic that’s convenient for maids and the like.

"We’ll have it checked at the church tomorrow. Tell Mary."

It seems Father will tell the butler. Why do I need to tell Mary? When I 'googled' it, various things became clear.

First, preparing my going-out clothes. And since Mary, as my lady's maid, has to come with me, she needs to prepare for that. There’s only Mary among the maids in the house, so if we go out, other chores have to be done in advance.

"Mary, I’m going to the church with Father tomorrow. It seems I’m going to have my magical aptitude examined."

Mary, who was cleaning, started bustling about, saying it was a big deal.

"My lady, your coat... let's try it on."

I tried on the coat that was hanging in the wardrobe in the room; it was a tight fit, but it was somehow alright.

"We will have to have a new coat made before school begins."

Mary let out a deep sigh. It didn't seem like we had the money for that. I wonder what we'll do?

The blue going-out outfit was brought from somewhere. The fabric was sturdier than what I was wearing now, and it looked warm.

"Please try this on. It might be a little big. I think it should be fine if you cinch it with a sash..."

Apparently, it was a hand-me-down from a relative's daughter. It was warmer than what I had now, so I had no complaints.

"Let's take in the shoulders a little."

Mary pinned it in a few places and carefully helped me take it off. She would surely fix it by tomorrow.

Although it was a last-minute effort, I decided that instead of just relying on Patience, I would look into daily life magic. And the church, too.

Even if I Google Patience's memories, not much comes up about the church. Just the sad memory of holding the funeral for her mother, Julianne.

It seems the Granger family isn't very religious. I don't know if everyone in the Kingdom of Laurence is lacking in faith, but personally, I couldn't handle hardcore believers, so that's probably fine.

Even if they aren't religious, it seems ability checks are done at the church.

『In ability checks in light novels and the like, the reincarnated person usually discovers a cheat ability like being able to use all magic, which causes a huge uproar, but...』

Becoming a prince's bride because of that sounds exhausting and I'd like to pass, but I do have hopes that if I have some level of cheat ability, I might be able to improve my current situation. Otherwise, I might starve to death. No, that might be an exaggeration...

I want to let my younger brothers have better meals. Of course, I need them too, and my skeletal father needs them as well. Thinking about such things, I arrived at the library, which is splendid despite our poverty.

The moment I voiced the thought, "If I sold the books here...", I got a terrible headache with a mental 『No!』.

"I'm joking," I placated Patience's memory. This is truly troublesome. From now on, whenever I do something a young lady shouldn't do for the sake of the Granger family, am I going to be stopped?

『Well, in that case,

I'll

persuade Patience that it's for the sake of my younger brothers.』

It seems Patience is quite the brother-con, or rather, she had been showering them with love and taking care of them ever since her mother passed away. And Patience herself is only 10 years old.

I wanted to complain a little to my father, who lacks life skills, but it seems she swore to her mother when she was dying.

"I will properly take care of Nashius and Henry."

My chest tightens at the sad memory. Even without such a vow, I would look after my cute younger brothers anyway.

"Life magic... church..."

Except for the wall with the windows, the books are packed right up to the ceiling. Reading the spines, I choose ones that seem likely to contain information about life magic and the church.
