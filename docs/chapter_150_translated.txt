Will I make it in time for the Aoba Festival?

Even when returning to the dormitory, I have a lot of luggage. I had George carry it to the dormitory dining hall. From there, Mary and I carried it up in several trips. Well, the groceries were already gone, so it's easier than before.

My Aoba Festival dress and hair accessories are already done, and I bought new shoes too. I’ve written new songs for the Music Club and have practiced the accompaniment for the Glee Club. In other words, the Alchemy Club is the one that's in trouble.

「Maybe a bicycle alone would have been enough?」

I’m anxious because I don’t know how much ice cream I need to make. But there were plenty of other students in the middle school division more anxious than I am.

「Patience, the dress doesn't seem like it will be finished in time. I might have to add another 2 hours.」

As soon as she arrived at the dormitory, Princess Margaret voiced her anxiety. If she doesn't dedicate the free 4th periods on Mondays and Fridays to sewing, it doesn't look like it will be finished in time. Four times a week for sewing is tough. Especially since it’s not something I even like. It would be fine if it were music class.

「How is it for the others?」

Since I’m only doing it once a week, I don't know the current situation.

「Everyone is panicking. They haven't even finished the base dress yet.」

Perhaps the teachers also misjudged the students' abilities.

「I wish there was something I could do to help...」

The teachers might forgive me for pointing out that Princess Margaret is mistaken, but I am not allowed to directly intervene. Having to sew 6 dresses is brutal. Especially since the Harvest Festival dress also requires a lining. I thought that, after all, I have no choice but to make a sewing machine, but right now the Alchemy Club is already fully occupied with the ice cream mock shop for the Aoba Festival and the bicycle test ride preparations.

「Come to think of it, the hemming was difficult...」

Even for me, the hemming of the New Look-style dress took quite a while to finish. It’s unacceptable for Princess Margaret to attend the Aoba Festival dance party in a dress with unhemmed edges. In my previous life, there was a stick-type glue for hemming. You apply it and fix it with an iron. When I moved out to live alone and bought curtains, they were a little too long, so I applied the glue and fixed them with an iron. Even after washing, it didn't come off.

If the Alchemy Club wasn't so busy, I could get help with the glue, but there's no such leeway right now. It seems like it could be made with slime powder and something else... what was glue made of again? In this other world, there are books. That means they are bound with something.

When I checked the books in my room, sure enough, they were held together by some kind of adhesive.

「I wish I could hold fabric together with this adhesive...」

It would be easier to apply if it were in a stick type. That part would be fine to solidify with slime powder. The problem is whether I have the time to do such experiments.

Monday afternoon is for the Alchemy Club.

Even though they said, "You're late," I came right after lunch. I walk slowly, so I can't help it.

「Patience, have you finished the ice cream recipe?」

I hand the recipe to Club President Caesar.

「Hmm? You didn't heat it during the trial production...」

「Yes, if I make it, I can purify the eggs, so it's fine without heating, but if we're selling the ice cream maker, it's safer to heat it. So, how many servings are you thinking of?」

Club President Caesar grunts, "Hmm."

「We don't know how many students will come. For the bicycles, we decided on 10 minutes per person. This is simple since we just need to make and place numbered tickets...」

Benjamin is also thinking on the sidelines. These two are really always at the Alchemy Club, aren't they?

「It would be better to make plenty. If there's any left, the members will buy it.」

I have the two of them come to the dormitory to pick up the luggage. They can't enter the girls' dormitory, so I make many trips up and down the stairs to bring the luggage down to the dining hall.

「Patience, why don't you ask the dorm maid?」

Benjamin is dumbfounded by the inefficiency. Well, if I tipped the maid, she would carry it, but I’m stingy.

「From now on, let's carry the luggage from the Alchemy Club. Here, I'll give you a key.」

From the Alchemy Club, George can carry it. It will be easier.

「Is it okay for me to keep the key?」

In the Music Club, Club President Albert and Vice President Rupert have keys. I thought the Alchemy Club keys were held by Club President Caesar and Benjamin.

「Yeah, Caesar and I are almost always at the Alchemy Club, anyway.」

I wonder about that, but I decided to keep the key until the Aoba Festival.

「I think we should make the ice cream beforehand and store it in a freezer. If it's fine for there to be leftovers, I'll make a large quantity.」

I have to think about what size to make the freezer. I'll leave that to Club President Caesar and Benjamin.

「Club President Caesar, is the glue used to bind books, etc., made of animal glue?」

I ask this after carrying the luggage into the clubhouse and tidying up. I need to ask quickly, because otherwise, they might focus on other topics.

「Probably... Patience, are you thinking about something again?」

Club President Caesar asked me back.

「Yes, I am thinking of a glue that doesn't require sewing... but I don't know if I can do it well.」

It mustn't soak through to the surface of the fabric. Also, it shouldn't peel off easily. The Alchemy Club has a lot to do for the Aoba Festival, but I can't leave the students who will be in trouble if their dresses aren't finished.

「Patience, you can focus on that. You probably need it for your home economics course anyway.」

I am sketching designs for glass containers and such.

「If you provide the sample for the glass containers, garden tables, and chairs, we will do the rest.」

Since he said that, I create the glass containers, garden tables, and chairs using alchemy.

「Patience, you could make a living on this alone.」

Benjamin praised me, but I'll leave the mass production to them.

Club President Caesar promised to prepare the animal glue, but I don't know if I can make it work well.

During Tuesday's sewing class, I check how far along Princess Margaret’s dress is. I’m relieved to find that the basting is finished.

「If it's just the main stitching, it seems you might make it in time.」

There were still students in the classroom who hadn't even finished basting.

「Yes, it was helpful that Patience wrote the order of sewing in chalk so I didn't make mistakes.」

I see, the students who hadn't finished basting yet were making mistakes and having to undo their work.

「The main stitching follows the same order as the basting. However, it won't finish beautifully unless you make the stitches fine.」

Princess Margaret, who was slow even with the rough basting stitches, only moves forward bit by bit now that it's main stitching. Sewing 6 dresses like this is ascetic practice. I decided that once the Aoba Festival is over, I will seriously make a sewing machine.

But right now, I have to make glue to stick the fabric together! Dancing in a dress with these kinds of stitches is too dangerous. It might even be necessary to hold the seam allowance in place. In other words, I have to make a glue that absolutely won't show through to the outer fabric. I was naively thinking that it would be okay if it were just for the hem, but the bar has been raised.

Club President Caesar isn't there on Wednesday morning, but since I have a key, I hole up in the clubhouse. He had left the animal glue for me.

I used animal glue in art class as well, but canvas is thick. Summer dress fabric is thin. I dissolve slime powder in water and mix it with the animal glue. I write the proportions in my notebook and mix them. Even after lunch, I continued to mix it.

「Patience, how is it?」

Benjamin called out to me, but it’s not reaching the desired firmness easily.

「It’s not reaching the ideal firmness easily, and besides, some degree of smoothness is also necessary...」

In reality, it’s something that takes months to make. I’m anxious about whether I can do it in a few weeks.

「You should step away and think about it for a while. Sometimes you can't see things when you focus too much.」

Except for the 1st period, I spent the whole day mixing animal glue and slime powder. My eyes are tired, and my shoulders are stiff.

「Yes, I'll take a short break.」

The clubhouse was lined with many garden tables and chairs. They are exactly according to the samples I made.

「If you paint these, they will be finished. I think white or dark green would be good.」

Glass containers are also being made in significant numbers. The ice cream maker and freezer are also in the process of being built.

「I wonder if we'll make it in time for the Aoba Festival.」

Club President Caesar laughs at an anxious me.

「Patience's dress is being sewn, isn't it? As for the students who don't make it in time, the teachers will find a way.」

That may be true, but...

「If you let me sew it, it would be done immediately.」

I couldn't help but complain.

「That wouldn't be a lesson, would it?」

Everyone laughed, but still, I want to easily finish hemming with glue.

「What kind of glue do you want to make?」

Club President Caesar intends to help me.

「But you also have the Aoba Festival preparations...」 as I hesitate to speak, Benjamin laughs at me.

「You are the only one who can make ice cream, Patience. If you don't hurry and finish making the glue, we'll be the ones cracking the eggs.」

Indeed, it seems cooking is impossible for this group. Starting from there, everyone worked to formulate a glue that sets firm with an iron and cannot be removed even by washing, yet applies smoothly and doesn't show through the fabric.

"Thank you very much."

It was fine even when washed in the washing machine. Plus, it doesn't show through the fabric. And as a byproduct, I also made a stick glue container. This could also be repurposed as a container for lipstick, couldn't it? The current ones come in small pots and are applied with a lip brush. I know this because I saw one on Princess Margaret's vanity. Let's try making lipstick. It seems like it could be profitable, so I'm happy.
