Glee Club

I have to deal with the cape matter thoroughly. Just filtering through that mess of documents to select the useful ones is going to be a major task.

Clearing my head, I head to the Music Club.

The Glee Club part is done since I finished composing it, and we have a few new songs, so it should be fine, right? I was thinking such naive thoughts, but reality is harsh after all.

"Patience, you've finished the new songs, haven't you? Then, we're going to the Glee Club now."

Eh, why do I have to go to another club?

"Albert, you've finished the lyrics!"

Princess Margaret shows no sign of stopping; in fact, she looks like she's going to lead the way.

"It’s finally finished, so it’s time for the first rehearsal. It’s better to have the composer there, after all. I might need to rearrange some impossible high notes."

Chairman Albert could do at least that much himself, couldn't he? I don't want to get into trouble with the Chorus Club. Louise is scary, after all.

"Well, the Glee Club shares the clubhouse with the Chorus Club, don't they? Even though they have enough members to be a club, Student Council President Rufus is slow to act."

It's certainly a problem to keep making them use the same clubhouse when there are vacant ones available. Moreover, a club that has split internally can't be comfortable to stay in. I was thinking about it as if it were someone else's business.

"Hmm? Aren't there too many people for the Glee Club?"

Before Chairman Albert could even voice his doubt, there were many female students there. I think they are members of the Chorus Club. After all, Louise is there too. Wow, I want to run away. I have a bad feeling that there’s going to be a conflict.

"Oh, Albert. Thanks for coming."

That must be Chairman Marks of the Glee Club. With his dark blond hair and brown eyes, he's a standard handsome guy. According to my tastes, he's a bit too well-bred, though.

"Marks, what is the meaning of this? I heard it was the first rehearsal, but are you having a joint performance with the Chorus Club?"

Ah, if he says something like that, it’s going to turn into a fight. Just as I thought, a female student who looked strong-willed glared at him sharply.

"This is the Chorus Club's clubhouse. I believe I said that members who quit are forbidden from entering, but it seems you have forgotten."

That's harsh. It's true that if you've left, it's technically correct to conduct activities in a different clubhouse. But the Student Council has had its hands full with the mess in the Knight Club. Well, even so, they are late.

"Chairman Emilia, aren't the Chorus Club's activities on Mondays and Wednesdays? That's why the Glee Club has scheduled Tuesdays and Thursdays, so why are you here on a Thursday?"

"We gathered for practice for the Aoba Festival. Do you have a problem with that?"

So they had divided up the days. And yet, out of spite, they gathered on a Thursday. How childish. Instead of engaging in such malicious behavior, it would be more proactive to practice earnestly. Well, I’m not that kind, so I won't say it. Plus, it's scary.

Thinking that the argument was about to heat up, I decided to withdraw from the Music Club. Chairman Albert doesn't seem like he’ll interfere in the fight between the Glee Club and the Chorus Club, either. Good.

I was thinking such naive things, but the sparks flew to the Music Club as well. Chairman Marks went to negotiate with the Student Council to have their clubhouse approved quickly, and finally, the Glee Club got its own clubhouse.

Things were fine up to here, but Chairman Emilia of the Chorus Club complained to the Student Council, asking why the Music Club is providing new songs only to the Glee Club. On top of that, she seemed to complain that the Dance Club is also cooperating with choreography. Are they free or what!

I suspect that Chairman Albert instigated Chairman Marks to create the Glee Club. That's why I don't want any sparks flying at me.

Since I’m going to the Royal Palace on Friday, I wrap up the hot water bottle and needle threader I made. I’m free during the third period, but Princess Margaret has embroidery.

"I want to experiment with slime powder in the Alchemy Club, but if I go, I don't think I'll be able to get back."

I decide to read the materials I received from Professor Kubrick. First, I glance through the materials and sort them. Materials about magic circles, materials about magic stones, and materials about embroidery thread.

I stack them into three rough piles. As I thought, there are few materials about embroidery thread. Surely, they won't have me embroider with spider silk like the templates in otherworld stories, will they? That’s impossible... wait, silk is also thread spat out by silkworms... is that possible?

While I was wondering where to start reading from the gathered piles, someone knocked on my dormitory room door.

The only people who come to my room are Princess Margaret or her maids, Sophie or Mary. Hmph, I have few friends, you see.

"Who is it?"

I don't know who it is, so I ask for a name.

"It's Louise."

Eh, Louise isn't a dorm student, is she? But since she's a female student, is she allowed to enter the girls' dormitory? I don't think Princess Margaret's school friends ever came to the dorms, either... well, it might be ungracious to refuse... though I have a bad feeling about this.

I say "Please come in" and open the door. Louise enters the room quickly. It seems she snuck in secretly to avoid being found by the dorm supervisor. Ah, it looks like my bad premonition is coming true.

"Good day," I say, probing her.

"Good day. Um, may I sit down?"

No, you may not. It feels like I'm about to get stuck in a mire. But Louise sits on the sofa without asking. She must be that desperate.

"I came today to talk about the Music Club."

Talking about the Music Club instead of the Chorus Club? Dark clouds are gathering.

"My, what could it be?" I play dumb.

"Chairman Marks of the Glee Club and Chairman Albert of the Music Club are in the same class, aren't they? Chairman Emilia is also in Class 5-A."

There are a lot of acquaintances in Class 5-A. Chairman Caesar and Percival are in the same class too. It was a topic I wanted to let fly over my head. In short, Louise is saying that it's been found out that Chairman Albert instigated Marks to set up the Glee Club.

But there shouldn't be any definitive proof. Even Chairman Albert wouldn't shout such a thing in homeroom, would he? Would he? No, I think it's fine. Louise is bluffing. I've been underestimated.

"My, I wouldn't know about that."

I can pretend to be a naive schoolgirl too. Besides, I don't really know how things are. I can only imagine.

"And the Music Club is providing new songs to the Glee Club and requesting cooperation from the Dance Club, right? Everyone is saying that this is the same as the Knight Club issue I mentioned earlier. I came to warn you because I thought it would be terrible if the Music Club, which Princess Margaret belongs to, were to be abolished."

That’s ridiculous. The Knight Club forced the Equestrian Club to take care of their horses and made the Magic Club accompany them during practice.

"Well, the Music Club simply provided new songs at the Glee Club's request. And the same goes for the Dance Club. Neither of them has suffered any disadvantage."

Louise's eyebrows rose slightly. She thought I was afraid of the club being abolished. Sorry to disappoint you.

"I'm very sorry, but I have to go to the Royal Palace today to accompany Princess Margaret. I have to get ready for that, so please take your leave."

I was glared at sharply by Louise. She has been dissatisfied from the beginning as to why I was chosen as Princess Margaret's attendant.

"That song is suitable for the Chorus Club. Mixing a song offered to God with such a trite love story is blasphemy."

The truth came out. She probably saw the Glee Club's sheet music or heard them practicing. "Amazing Grace" is a masterpiece, after all, and Louise wants to sing it.

"Lady Louise, how about switching to the Glee Club? The Chorus Club is all female students, and there are few female students in the Glee Club."

I think Louise is quite good at singing. But the Chorus Club is a world of women. The seniority and class system ties seem strict. Louise is a count's daughter, so she’s high in the caste, but she might not get a solo during her primary school years. Even if she could sing a solo,

it would clash with the Knight Club’s matches.

"That kind of thing... that song is perfect for me, even though..."

Yes, yes, please do your worrying at your own mansion. I finally managed to see her out.

Louise is greedy. She doesn't want to live in the dorms, but she wants to be Princess Margaret's attendant. She wants to sing good songs, but she doesn't want to switch to the Glee Club, which has a short history.

Born as a count's daughter, gifted with light magic, excellent grades, and beautiful too. I think she has nothing to complain about, but there are things that the person herself cannot tolerate. I don't want to get involved with such a selfish young lady.
