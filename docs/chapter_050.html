Nashius's birthday

Usually, Sophie the lady's maid accompanies me, but today, since I have a letter from the Queen, a court lady is also coming along. Charlotte Evans is the court lady who delivered the harpsichord to me before.

"Lady Charlotte, I have something I would like to ask you. Would that be alright?"

Charlotte has her brown hair braided and pinned up without a single strand out of place, and she wears a black court lady's uniform. She has an unapproachable air about her, but if I don't ask, I'll be in trouble later.

"Queen Victoria said she would prepare the necessities at the summer palace, but I think there are some things I should bring myself. I don't have a mother, so I have to instruct my lady's maid. Please, tell me."

"The Queen will certainly have the necessities for living at the summer palace prepared. Clothes, dresses, nightgowns, and undergarments as well. However, you might find it more comfortable to bring the clothes, nightgowns, and undergarments you are used to wearing. Since the summer palace is by the sea, you will also need old clothes to use as swimwear. I think it would also be convenient to bring casual clothes for playing outside."

In other words, high-class dresses, clothes, nightgowns, and one set of undergarments will be provided, but I will likely need changes of clothing. Plus, old clothes for swimming and clothes for playing outside. I'm glad I asked.

"Thank you. Also, must I bring a lady's maid with me?"

Lady Charlotte looks surprised.

"If you do not have a lady's maid, the Queen will likely assign one to you, but I think it is more comfortable to be tended to by a lady's maid of your own choosing."

Well, obviously, I'd rather have Mary than be tended to by a stranger. But I only have Mary at home.

"Um, what should a lady's maid bring?"

"The lady's maid will know that herself."

That's right, I'll leave it to Mary. The carriage arrives at the house, the court lady Charlotte hands the Queen's letter to Wyatt, and Sophie the maid hands over a basket containing eggs and other items.

May my father decline the trip to the summer palace! It's a hollow wish, so I won't pray to God.

What will be, will be, so I pick red tomatoes, eggplants, and cucumbers in the greenhouse with my younger brothers. I pick three small-looking, bright red tomatoes, clean them until they're cold and beautiful, and bite into one.

"Freshly picked tomatoes are delicious, aren't they?"

"Yeah, I love tomatoes."

Ah, if only I could spend the summer vacation like this.

Seeing my father's happy face at the dinner table, I realized that the trip to the summer palace had been decided.

"Patience, because you have been serving Princess Margaret diligently, Queen Victoria has decided to take you to the summer palace. It is a gracious offer."

So he isn't going to decline after all. How disappointing.

"Father, but I intended to spend the summer vacation with my younger brothers. Besides, even if I am told I may bring a lady's maid, I only have Mary."

As if struck by a thought, my father opened his gray eyes wide.

"Is that so... only Mary. This house has become lonely, hasn't it?"

This is no time for him to be wallowing in sentimentality about the house being lonely. I don't need a lady's maid to come with me anymore. I live by myself in the dormitory anyway.

My father, who still lacked any practical life skills, had told me, "Take a maid with you," but the manor would be in trouble if Mary were gone. I should consult with Wyatt.

The dinners have improved quite a bit. The soup is packed with vegetables and beans. The bread is, well, a little softer. The meat consists of thinly sliced, grilled chicken breast laid out on a plate, topped with tomato sauce. And for dessert, there are crepes with strawberry sauce. I gave the recipe to Eva. The thinness of the crepes is truly impressive.

"This is the first time I've eaten this."

Ah, that's right, it's the first crepe in this other world.

"I gave Eva a recipe that thins out the previous pancake recipe and had her make it. It's a dessert called a crepe."

I wondered if my younger brothers had been given some to eat too, as I ate the crepe with my father. The tea has become a little stronger. It's still thin, though.

After dinner, I talked with my younger brothers in the nursery for a short while.

"Sister, I can do a double jump now."

"Nashius, you worked hard."

"I can do consecutive double jumps now."

"Henry, you worked hard too."

I kissed them both and said, "Goodnight." Happiness! Sigh, I was supposed to be able to live like this throughout my summer vacation.

On Saturday, I made Nashius's birthday cake from the morning. Eva was the one making it, but I used life magic to help whip the egg whites and heavy cream. We cut the sponge cake in the middle and spread heavy cream and strawberry jam on it.

"Decorate the top of the cake with eight pieces of strawberry jam that still have chunks of fruit in them. There's enough for everyone, so please eat it."

I wish I had a pastry bag and decorating tips for the cream. But we managed to make a wonderful birthday cake with bright red strawberries on top of a pure white cake. Since we don't have thin candles for birthdays, I stood one that was as thin as possible on top.

The birthday cake was served for dessert at lunch.

"Happy Birthday!"

Nashius looked surprised, his grey eyes wide. Henry's blue eyes were round, too.

"This is amazing. Patience, you have a talent for cooking."

I'm happy to be praised, but it's Nashius's birthday.

"Now, Nashius, make a wish while you blow out the candle."

Nashius looked puzzled. It seems like a custom that doesn't exist in this world.

"It's popular among the girls at the Royal Academy. Now, blow it out!"

He puffed out his cheeks and blew it out. As I clapped my hands, Henry, Father, and also Mary, Wyatt, Eva, and George clapped along.

"Sister, this is my first time having a birthday like this."

Nashius kissed me. I'm truly happy! I really wanted to spend the summer vacation together.
