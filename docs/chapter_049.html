Final Exams

The academy, which had been buzzing with excitement for the Aoba Festival, regained its composure once the scope for the June final exams was announced.

I’ve been busy with my kitchen garden on the weekends. Since the strawberry season is over, I’m planting tomatoes, eggplants, and cucumbers in the greenhouse. (Well, George is, anyway.)

"Grow big!" I use life magic to make them grow. After all, tomatoes, eggplants, and cucumbers aren’t in the grocery stores yet. In other words, they’re expensive. Oh, I don’t walk around town anymore. I heard it from Eva, who goes to buy groceries. Because seeing Mary was too hard for me to bear.

"Let's eat half at home. But we'll sell the rest."

The reason why British aristocrats in my previous life served cucumber sandwiches for tea was that cucumbers were expensive because they couldn't be grown without a greenhouse. In other words, cucumbers can be sold at a high price. Yes!

Besides that, I also harvest beans, cabbage, potatoes, and carrots from the backyard. I help my younger brothers with this a little bit.

Mary doesn't seem to approve, but she seems to find fun things within the acceptable range.

We won't sell these. They can be preserved, and I'll even make sauerkraut with the cabbage.

After finishing the harvest, I mix compost fertilizer into the field. (George made the compost for me.) I'm really glad I can use life magic. Once the ridges are finished, I have George plant the beans, potatoes, and tomatoes. With the coming season, it looks like I can grow tomatoes in the open ground too.

I also cast "Grow big!" on the fruit trees. They’ve blossomed, so I hope they’ll bear fruit too.

I also take care of the roses in the front garden. Since I’m taken to the royal palace once or twice a month and dropped back off, it would be embarrassing if it were too desolate. Besides, Patience seems happy about it, too.

"When the final exams are over, it's summer vacation! I'll be with

my younger brothers

the whole time."

I have no problems except for the mathematics certificate. I've already studied quite a bit for that too. So, on weekends, I spend my days fully immersed in the kitchen garden and my nights fully immersed in side work.

"Summer is wonderful! The evenings are bright, too."

It saves on candles as well. But I mustn't get carried away. A harsh winter will come again. I never want to experience cold and hunger ever again.

"I have to get Wyatt to buy firewood... Horses are far away."

At the Aoba Festival, not only the members of the equestrian club but also Prince Keith and the others were accustomed to horses. If things go on like this, Nashius will fall behind in physical education. Being a boy who is bad at physical education seems like it would be embarrassing.

It can't be helped that food and firewood are the top priority, but I want more money.

"It would be great if I could get a patent or something in the Alchemy Club..."

When I told Princess Margaret I wanted to join the Alchemy Club, she made a difficult face.

"If you have time to join a club full of weirdos like that, why don't you put effort into getting your dance certificate or compose a new song?"

I decided to join the club only after I learned alchemy in class. I knew from reading books in the mansion that there are people who can become alchemists and people who cannot.

"The magic for metalworking is fire and earth, right? It's probably easier if you can use water too. I only have life magic, after all."

But, because my life magic is a little cheat-like, maybe I can do it. If I can skip a grade to the middle division next year, I'll try taking the class.

The final exams were a breeze. Well, they were only at an upper elementary to middle school level, after all. For math, I was the only one who took the 3rd-year middle school exam in a separate room. I think I solved every problem. I checked for calculation errors several times, so it should be fine. Goddess Estena, please let me pass!

The exams were returned, and I got almost a perfect score.

I even got a certificate of completion for math! Now I want completion certificates for Japanese, history, classics, and magic studies too. Before, I had to rely on Patience, but now I can do it myself.

Next year I’ll have two courses, so it’ll be busy. But it’ll be easier if the required subjects are exempted. I’ll work harder on my side job so I can buy a horse. After all, Nashius will be turning nine. I’ll try making a birthday cake for his birthday (well, Eva will). I’m being summoned by the Queen today anyway, so I should be able to get ingredients like eggs.

Perhaps it was karma for thinking such selfish things.

"Patience, let’s go to the summer palace together. Margaret would enjoy that too."

Just like always, after art class ended on Friday, Sophie came to pick me up from the royal palace. Heading to the royal palace with her was the same as always, as was playing the Hanon.

While I was having delicious tea, Queen Victoria dropped a bombshell.

"Mother, thank you. I was worried Jane would be bored because she’s still young."

I want to decline. After all, I’m supposed to spend summer vacation with my younger brothers. Besides, I have to grow food for winter in the fields during the summer.

I gathered my courage and looked at the Queen to decline. Ah, I can’t decline. I realized it the moment I looked into her eyes. Whether it’s the Queen or Princess Margaret, they both give instructions with their eyes. I can come up with various excuses for words, but I can’t go against a gaze.

"Thank you. I will consult with my father and give you an answer."

Queen Victoria smiled with satisfaction. She knows Viscount Granger won’t refuse.

"We will provide everything you need for your stay at the detached palace, so you don’t need to worry about anything. You may bring your lady-in-waiting if you wish to bring one."

The Queen doesn't even consider that I only have Mary at home.

"I will consult with my father."

Oh, since it’s summer vacation, I won’t be in my uniform. Wait, could it be that I’ll be eating dinner and such with them? With the King too? I want to eat in the nursery with Princess Jane and Prince Marcus. But I gave up, knowing that everything depends on Queen Victoria’s will.

Inside the magnificent royal carriage, I wish my father would refuse. But that seems unlikely.
