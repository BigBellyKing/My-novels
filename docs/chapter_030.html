Being a lady-in-waiting is truly difficult.

Being a lady-in-waiting for Princess Margaret is tough. When I go to her room after school, I am immediately given tasks.

"I was given so much math homework."

Certainly, there were many problems on the printout, but it was only one page. Is that a lot? It would be faster to just tell her the answers, but that won't do. I let Princess Margaret solve them, and when she gets stuck, I give her hints about the formulas to use. It took quite a while, but she finished all the problems.

"Patience, I'm tired. Make some tea. Brew some for yourself, too."

The special room has a small kitchen attached. I brew tea using the gold-rimmed tea set that Zofie, the maid, brought out. I loved tea in my past life as well, and since the tea leaves were high-quality, I was able to brew it quite aromatically.

"You're good at making tea. Do you make your own tea at the Granger house?"

At home, I only drink the weak tea that Eva makes. I haven't been able to improve the quality of the tea leaves in my daily life yet. Luxury items have a low priority, after all.

"No, but I know how to brew it as a hobby." That's not a lie, though it was in my past life.

"Oh, please practice this song."

I was handed some sheet music. It seems to be a song that even Patience doesn't know.

"Is this a new piece?"

"That's right, the music club is creating new songs, isn't it wonderful? I want you to compose a new song for me, too, Patience."

There are some parts that are slightly different from the sheet music of my past life, but I read and play it using Patience's knowledge.

After eating dinner together, next was reading books aloud. It seems Hanon was taken into consideration since it was nighttime.

I want a CD player and a TV. Sigh

The next morning, I woke up before the bell rang. This is because I have to wake up Princess Margaret and help her get ready.

"Princess Margaret, please wake up." No matter how many times I said it, she wouldn't wake up. Is there no way to wake her up with life magic? I pulled memories from my past life, thinking that being weak in the morning due to low blood pressure is caused by poor blood circulation.

"Wake up!"

I cast the spell, imaging her blood circulation improving, thinking it might not work. She woke up.

"Good morning."

"Good morning. Patience, what did you do? I woke up feeling very refreshed."

Despite saying that, she is still lounging in bed. I remembered a British historical drama. In the morning, a maid would bring morning tea. Let's try waking up her body with a warm drink.

To save time, I cast "Produce hot water!" and poured hot water into the pot. Ah, as expected, high-quality tea leaves have a wonderful aroma. From tomorrow on, I will make sure to brew the tea before waking her up.

"Princess Margaret, please drink this."

While Princess Margaret is drinking tea in bed, I cast life magic on her: "Become beautiful!"

"Once you finish your tea, please get changed."

"It would be nice if you could use life magic to change my clothes, too."

While saying things like that, she finished her tea, got out of bed looking sharp, and changed her clothes. Good grief, now I just need to fix her hair.

"Clang, clang."

I was relieved to be finished getting her ready before the breakfast bell rang this morning.

"I feel hungry for some reason. Is it because I drank tea? That's strange."

"Perhaps your body has awakened thanks to the warm drink."

Princess Margaret, who barely ate any breakfast yesterday, had finished almost everything. I finished my meal, too.

"Today is music club, so come to the classroom once class is over."

I want to complain about how demanding they are, but this is also

for the sake of

my younger brothers. I wonder why my father was dismissed from his post. The teachers seem to think highly of me, and the Queen thought well enough of me to make me her lady-in-waiting, so she probably doesn't have a bad opinion of me, right? Well, that might just be because I was the only girl from a viscount’s family or higher living in the dormitory.

Tuesday is Classics, Mathematics, Magic Studies, and Dance. I really need to study a bit more for Classics. Patience is seriously brilliant, but it’s pathetic of me to keep relying on her. I want to get my certificate for Mathematics as soon as possible.

For lunch, for some reason, even Prince Keith chose the fish. I wonder if he just copied Prince Richard because he chose the fish? Of course, I had the fish.

"It's not that I can't eat fish," Prince Keith says, acting all cheeky. He has a rather cute personality. I’m glad everyone rated it highly. It would be painful to think they were eating something tasteless while pretending, "It's delicious!"

In Magic Studies, many students had skipped a grade. Both second-years and first-years. Don't you think there's a need to change the class content? I found myself thinking things like that, and other things too. Yes, about sweets. Today's dessert was also too sweet; it reached a level where even I, as much as I love sweets, gave up on finishing it. It’s a waste of expensive sugar and eggs. I want to eat cookies and cakes made to my own taste. I feel like if I taught Eva the recipes, she could make them, but that would require money.

As for Dance... I don't want to talk about it. Since becoming a second-year, there isn't a single student I’m close to. Well, I wasn't particularly close to anyone in my first year either, though. Louise, who skipped a grade, was popular here as well. As for me, I'm just a short, studying nerd, anyway. Since there are fewer girls, there are partners available, but I couldn't get paired with anyone good at it. That is miserable. Because with dancing, if your partner is good at leading, you can manage somehow. I said I didn't want to talk about it, but I ended up complaining anyway.
